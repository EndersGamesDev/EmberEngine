# Detailed cell props

Seven runtime props total **2,217,328 bytes and 26,308 triangles**. Their embedded textures total **998,252 bytes**. Every GLB has one mesh, one primitive, one base-color image, opaque material, white base-color factor, POSITION/NORMAL/TEXCOORD_0, and no node transform. The embedded atlases are RGB8 JPEG at quality 93 without chroma subsampling; editable RGB PNG atlases remain beside them. The checked local gltf 1.4.1 import dependency enables JPEG and PNG, and both decode to the engine's supported RGB8 format. Expected total texture VRAM with mips is about 12.33 MiB; repeating an existing mesh instance adds no texture copy.

All dimensions, exact exported bounds, named animation anchors and SHA-256 hashes are in `props-manifest.json`. Floor props are normalized to Y=0. Chain and rag retain their top pivots, including negative Y bounds. Wall props face +Z. The cot's headboard is at positive Z and its mattress surface is approximately Y=0.66. Place it unrotated at (-2.25,0,3.55) for the existing cell arrangement: X bounds approximately [-2.763,-1.735], Z bounds [2.50,4.60]. The few straw fibers beyond the frame need no added collision.

The cot is built from beveled and pegged timber rails, slats, iron nails, a rounded linen mattress and pillow, raised stitching, escaping straw fibers and folded cloth. The bucket has individually tapered hollow staves, a wooden bottom, two wide iron bands, rivets and a bail handle. The shackle hangs from alternating elliptical chain links, an end connector and a riveted wall anchor. The rag's folds, torn hem and holes are geometry, not alpha. The grate has beveled forged bars and hex bolts. The stool has individual seat boards, bracing and nail heads; its tin saucer holds a deformed candle with wax drips, puddles and a charred wick.

Animation is intentionally separate from the baked geometry. Sway chain or rag about their origin by small X/Z rotations, approximately 0.02–0.04 radians; this preserves the wall/hem position. Candle flame center is local (0.003,0.927987,-0.002); use the particle channel for flame/smoke and place a flickering local light near Y=0.96. The flame is not included as an opaque mesh. Bucket handle axis is documented at (0,0.352,0), but the handle is part of the single mesh and cannot be rotated independently without a future split. These suggestions are presentation transforms, not pre-baked animation clips.

Reproduce from the parent runtime-v2-assets directory: run `generate_prop_textures.py` with Python + numpy/Pillow; run Blender 5.2 background with four threads and Idle priority against `build_props.py`; then run `finalize_props.py` with Python + numpy/Pillow/scipy. `build_props.py -- bucket` regenerates one selected prop; multiple short names may follow `--`. Editable packed `.blend` files and the standalone RGB PNG atlases are retained. Reproduction uses seed 28092026 plus each prop name. The material generator consumes the root-supplied `surfaces/oak-aged-albedo-v2.png` when present; this picture came from built-in ImageGen, and its exact path/hash are recorded in the manifest. Each wooden face samples one crop with grain aligned along its long dimension. Iron, linen, straw and wax sources are generated deterministically by local code. Base color and subtle contact AO are baked to new UV atlases, then black gutters receive neighboring color to avoid dark mip seams.
