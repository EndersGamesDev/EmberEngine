# End Game V10 generated enemies and audio

Three independently generated textured body bases supply the castle soldier, hollow knight and One-Eyed Castellan. SDXL concepts and TRELLIS.2 source meshes were produced with the existing Specht and Adler workers, then converted offline into named rigid parts. Raw sources remain outside the repository under `runtime-v10-assets`; only runtime assets and provenance belong here.

Each body GLB uses 18 `enemy_*` nodes: pelvis, torso, neck, head, and paired upper arms, forearms, hands, thighs, shins, boots and coat panels. The models use +Y up, -Z facing, identity node transforms and standing bind coordinates normalized to 1.865 m. Rig sidecars define the part hierarchy and pivots. The soldier and hollow knight reuse the established warden bind; the broad cyclops uses wider arm and leg pivots while retaining the same names and vertical lengths. The renderer supplies the final gameplay heights.

Each generated body is segmented and rebuilt as closed convex part envelopes, with generated facial relief projected back onto the head. This retopology removes contradictory internal surfaces and fragments, but simplifies armor folds, fingers and concave silhouettes. Compact RGB8 textures are rebaked from the original generated sculpt. The cyclops's ambiguous brow is repaired with one recessed dark central eye beneath the generated brow, baked into its head atlas; `oneeye_local` is an absolute standing bind coordinate. Joint and inner leg covers, rigid segmentation and eye repair are authored. These are rigid articulated meshes, not skinned or motion-captured characters; single-view generation infers hidden surfaces.

Separate `weapon-sword.glb`, `weapon-spear.glb`, `weapon-axe.glb` and `weapon-maul.glb` are authored measured meshes. Each has one opaque RGB8 atlas and a grip at the origin, with shaft/blade along +X, broad blade normal +Y and cutting-edge width along Z. `weapons.json` records the primary/support grips and exact tips or contact points. These weapons are authored geometry rather than generated source meshes.

`voice-lines.js` exports `CASTLE_LINES` for `boss_intro`, `boss_phase2`, `boss_defeat`, `escape_clue` and `escape_ending`. The corresponding prerecorded PCM WAV files are mono, 24 kHz, signed 16-bit. Cached CPU Kokoro-82M produced the speech, using the recorded model revision and voice blends in `voice-manifest.json`. The inscription is softened with quiet breath-like processing; no particular actor or human performance is claimed.

`castle-ambience.wav` is a 16-second mono 48 kHz loop made with seeded offline DSP: periodic spectral wind, a low drone, damped metallic chain partials and circular echoes. It is explicitly authored audio rather than output from an audio generation model. Voice and ambience waveforms are checked for duration, hashes, finite samples and clipping; no listening or browser/device playback was performed by the asset worker.

Reproduction and verification tools are in `tools/end-game/v10/`. Per-body manifests record decoded geometry, texture residency including each part's mip chain, provenance, and central torso coverage so a nonempty fragment cannot silently substitute for the body. Native motion and whole-scene budgets are checked separately by the game integration.
