# League environment specifications

These JSON records define the prompts, exclusions, seeds, dimensions, step counts, and guidance values for the reviewed v4 moss, paving, and plaza source pictures.

`launch-assets.py` submits the specifications as durable generator jobs, and the environment bakers verify those job records before producing the surface GLBs consumed by League's v4 scene; the three moss variants document selection history rather than interchangeable runtime assets.

Accepted appearance, hashes, and the single seam-repair job are recorded in `docs/plans/ultimate-league-v4.md`, while `docs/asset-pipeline.md` governs provenance and retention of the raw pictures outside the shipped asset tree.
