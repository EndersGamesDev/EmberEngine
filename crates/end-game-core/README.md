# End Game simulation

Local single-player simulation in metres, kilograms and seconds. Gravity is 9.81 m/s². Material densities, sliding friction, restitution, roughness and metallicity are explicit prototype constants; density times solid box volume gives mass, impulse changes velocity inversely with mass, and floor impacts accumulate cosmetic wear. The player uses a kinematic movement controller, not a full ragdoll. These constants are representative starting values, not calibrated physical measurements.

The fixed-step state machine covers floorboard, key, cell lock, sword, armor and exit. The warden wakes from proximity, running, sustained attack noise or damage. Collision constrains cell/corridor walls, bars and cot; the loose wooden crate responds to impulses. No networking or existing game's protocol is changed. Tests cover traversal from spawn without teleportation, progression, collision, mass, gravity, friction, diagonal movement, stealth and attack detection.

V3 interactions own their approach, contact, commit and recovery times. A collision-safe grounded approach precedes each pickup; competing movement, dodge, strike and repeated interact input cannot interrupt or queue behind it. Stage changes commit once after grasp/manipulation. The renderer consumes this state and the shared board pose without owning progression.

V4, Weight of the Blade, introduced `combat.rs` for the first five authored two-handed strikes while retaining those interaction contracts and the client's V3 articulated assets. One press starts Cut. Wolf's Fang is three quick presses selecting Cut → Backhand → Finisher; Gravebreaker is press, pause, press selecting Cut → Overhead; Rising Wolf is press, press, pause, press selecting Cut → Backhand → Rising. Quick means a gap of at most 0.30 seconds (18 fixed ticks); delayed means greater than 0.30 and at most 0.90 seconds (19–54 ticks). Gaps are measured between consecutive press edges. Holding attack supplies no new press edge.

Up to two valid follow-ups queue while the current strike completes. Each spends stamina when it starts and owns windup, an active contact window, follow-through and recovery. V11 replaces the original one-time range/cone damage selection with the shared swept blade described below. A confirmed hit freezes world motion for hitstop while the rhythm clock keeps accepting valid queued presses. Pickups cancel stale combat, and breaking the exit chain clears future strikes while retaining recovery.

V5 `warden.rs` owns the enemy’s Sleeping, Waking, Hunting, Attacking, Staggered and Dead states. The 1.65-second wake sequence precedes movement and knife damage. Each knife attack spends 0.50 seconds winding up, evaluates contact at 0.63 seconds and finishes recovery at 1.42 seconds. Facing is committed for the attack; damage requires current reach, facing, a clear path and no dodge. Pursuit respects corridor bounds and the sword plinth. Heavy sword hits can stagger an upright enemy; death cancels future attacks. Enemy motion and attack time freeze with the existing combat hitstop and client pause.

V6 `dialogue.rs` records eight recent voice events with per-life IDs. Real grounded, uncrouched footsteps in the cell may produce a movement bark every twelve unfrozen seconds. The lock and sword interaction commits produce one reaction each. Fatal contact or an observed dead warden produces one terminal death event. Prisoner respawn advances the life ID and resets dialogue history. Speech never changes combat or alert rules.

V8 `guard.rs` owns the held sword guard, raise/lower timing, stamina cost, break recovery and contact event counters. Blocking is directional and requires an equipped sword and grounded player after committed interactions and swings finish. The client maps mouse, keyboard, gamepad and touch holds to the same `Controls.block` value.

V9 `layout.rs` owns the shared castle solids, generated prop sockets, support selection and kinematic stair/ceiling collision. The far exit requires the sword, a defeated warden and a broken chain; `exit_open` animates the actual passage. Stage 5 continues exploring instead of showing a completion screen. `grounded`, location labels and the seven-place discovery count support elevated movement, combat and presentation. See `docs/end-game-castle.md` for the route and verification boundaries.

V10 `enemies.rs` owns the nine castle encounters, five weapon attacks, contact tests, guard costs, boss phase and shared attack scheduling. `quest.rs` owns discovered clues, the ordered Sun/Wolf/Bell vows, crown drop, three finite healing shrines, checkpoint recovery and physical sally-port completion. `finished()` requires walking out beyond the opened final gate. The bounded castle event history exports stable audio keys without consuming events during HUD reads. Combat remains deterministic at 60 Hz.

V11 `blade.rs` owns shared edge-leading choreography; `sword.rs` interpolates previous/current physical frames and sweeps the same blade through the fixed-step active window. The first three cuts descend upper-right to lower-left, cross left to right, then descend on a steeper diagonal. Five yaw-oriented target volumes distinguish head, left/right torso and left/right leg. Obstruction and substep contact select actual hit points, normals and tangents; one body hit is allowed per cut. Stored contact frames keep hitstop/rebound at the reached pose, including fast player turns and translation.

`Controls.attack` retains the normal rhythm buffer; `Controls.heavy` independently selects grounded Overhead or airborne JumpHeavy. The latter cuts downward in the air and holds its follow-through until landing, without a second landing damage event. Only one airborne heavy is allowed per jump. Guard rejects both request types. `HitReaction` carries directional per-zone offsets and the current reaction into subsequent hits; surface contact records material identity and up to 64 bounded events, cleared by checkpoint/full reset.

The body targets are pose-sampled proxies, not per-bone/deforming-mesh collision. Generated prop AABBs and moving gates can emit transient effects; persistent scratch candidates are restricted to structural planes. Sword contacts do not simulate moving-prop breakage, mesh deformation or calibrated biomechanics. Input mapping, reference links and validation boundaries are in `docs/end-game-combat.md`; rendering and audio remain client responsibilities.
