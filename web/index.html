Fight Arena

online multiplayer · first person · up to 8
First-person drop-in deathmatch in a generated arena. WASD + mouse look, Space jump, RMB zoom, R reload, Shift sprint, C crouch — crouching also shrinks your hitbox.

Fire Racer

local + online · 8 cars · 3 laps
Arcade racing through a gothic castle bailey — a 920 m circuit with a main straight, a long sweeper, a fountain chicane and a hairpin. WASD to drive, Space for the handbrake to break traction and drift, Shift to spend one of three boost charges. Practice alone, or open a lobby and race other people on an authoritative server.

Level builder

fly · place · move, rotate, scale · spawn points
Fly a level with the right mouse button held (WASD, Space/Ctrl for height, Shift to boost). Release it and W, E, R switch the gizmo between move, rotate and scale; left click selects. Digit keys arm a shape, Enter places it on the ground under your aim, X deletes. The three world axes are drawn in red, green and blue so you always know which way you are dragging.
native only, for now
The editor runs today as a desktop window: cargo run -p ember-editor --bin ember-editor-app. The browser build is next — the sidebar has to be page HTML, because the engine's debug UI is not compiled for the web at all.
