# End Game 9.0.0 — Beyond the Dungeon

Escape the basement, take the greatsword, defeat the warden and break the exit chain. The gate opens onto a lower passage and a monumental great hall. Climb the grand stair to the backyard garden, enter the tower and use its landings to reach the castle walls and summit. The castle stays open for exploration and backtracking. Location and discovery cues accompany the route; no new inhabitants are added in this version.

Generated stone supports, a dry fountain and a tower portal come from the SDXL/TRELLIS asset workers. Their measured bounds sit in a shared layout with floors, stair treads, landings and walls; that layout drives both rendering and collision. Source jobs, conversion settings and texture provenance are in `assets/end-game/v9/`.

Hold right mouse, F, L2, or the touch HOLD Guard button to defend with the equipped greatsword. The blade raises in 0.18 seconds and lowers in 0.12 seconds. Guard becomes available on the ground after interactions, dodges and committed swings finish. New attack presses during a held guard are discarded; previously committed combo swings finish first. Releasing guard lets stamina recover.

Face the incoming knife. A successful frontal block costs 28 stamina, prevents health damage, produces a brief hitstop and metallic contact sound, and gives a small controller pulse. Insufficient stamina breaks guard for 0.9 seconds and allows the normal knife damage, with a heavier sound and pulse. The HUD distinguishes raised guard, knife contact and a broken guard. Contact audio and the edge flash follow each simulation snapshot before the slower HUD refresh. Browser audio and controller vibration still depend on device support and user settings.

Mouse, keyboard and touch holds are tracked independently. Pointer release, cancellation, lost capture, blur, page hiding and pause clear the relevant held state. A controller trigger held across pause must be released before guard can raise again; a pause/resume between simulation updates also clears its intent. Right-click suppresses the canvas context menu and does not create an attack. Existing mouse/gamepad/touch strike rhythms, interactions, movement, camera controls, subtitles and voice playback remain available.

The V7 generated, textured jailer and articulated motion remain, including the scarred face, high collar, worn coat and boots. V6's four prerecorded voice reactions and subtitles remain: audible cell footsteps, unlocking the gate, claiming the sword and death. Movement barks have a twelve-second cooldown; crouching is quiet. One voice plays at a time, pause preserves its clip offset, and respawning clears old dialogue. Missing or blocked audio still displays subtitles.

Build with `tools/end-game/build.ps1`, serve `web/`, and open this directory. The scoped publisher writes V9 and preserves the frozen V1–V8 releases. Generated character provenance is under `assets/end-game/v7/`; retained speech assets are under `assets/end-game/v6/`.

Passive native guard captures use `END_GAME_SCENE=guard` with `END_GAME_GUARD_PHASE=raise`, `held`, `lower`, `recoil` or `break`. `END_GAME_ACTION_TIME` specifies seconds within that pose; it defaults to 0.06. Set the normal `EMBER_CAPTURE_PATH` and capture dimensions through the native harness. These frozen fixtures do not drive workstation input. Client tests cover device equivalence, guard release after pause, committed-combo preservation and contact feedback. `node --test tools/end-game/guard.test.mjs` executes the actual V9 shell handlers against passive DOM/API doubles for pointer cancellation, independent holds, pause clearing and event polling. These checks do not substitute for browser gameplay, physical device or measured frame-pacing validation.

Passive `END_GAME_SCENE=combat` captures also accept `END_GAME_PREVIOUS_STRIKE` and `END_GAME_NEXT_STRIKE`, using `cut`, `backhand`, `finisher`, `overhead` or `rising`. `END_GAME_LINK_TIME` sets both the previous strike's link time and the current buffered link time, clamped to the corresponding strike duration, and defaults to 0.1 seconds. These optional fields stage linked recovery and next-chamber presentation without changing gameplay input or combo rules.
