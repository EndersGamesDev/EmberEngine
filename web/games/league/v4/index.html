    Mouse and keyboard

      A safe first match

      1. Choose Practice 1v1 to face a bot, or Practice 3v3 for a full team fight. Online Quick match uses your selected team size; a password creates a private lobby.
      2. Pick a champion, select two different spells, and choose exactly three runes. Save different rune combinations on pages A, B and C. Teammates need different champions; opponents may mirror your pick.
      3. Start at level 1 with a skill point and 500 gold. Learn an ability with its +, then buy an item. Unlearned abilities cannot cast.
      4. Right-click down the lane, aim your learned ability at an enemy, and press its key. Fight beside your minions. Retreat to your core's fountain when low.
      5. Spend new points and gold as you earn them. Win by destroying the opposing core.

      Attacks, movement and casts

      Right-click a hostile unit to follow it and attack automatically whenever it is in reach and your attack is ready. Right-click clear ground to move there. Left-click operates buttons and HUD slots; it does not move or attack on the field.

      Attack-move travels toward your cursor and engages the nearest enemy champion, minion or core within 8 world units. It stays on that target until it dies or leaves that radius, then resumes the route. At the destination it keeps watching for enemies. Neutral Courts are excluded: right-click one explicitly. A new move, attack, or Stop order replaces attack-move.

      SW4RM recoils as it fires drones; EmberKnight lunges into a flame slash; the Hallow One rises to release a petal; Bogmaw drives a heavy claw; Tessera twists into a clockwork shot. Their reach and attack speed differ. Move after a strike or cast an ability to cut the visible recovery. This never resets the paid attack cooldown, grants an extra hit, or recalls a projectile already released.

      Abilities aim at the cursor. Check each kit below: some need an enemy, others affect an area or a living ally. An unavailable target, unlearned rank, insufficient mana or an active cooldown can prevent a cast. Health is the green bar; mana is the blue bar. The cooldown overlay shows when an action is ready again.

      Levels, runes and equipment

      Earn gold from kills, qualifying assists, minion last hits and passive income. Assists require damage to the victim within the previous eight seconds; healing or shielding alone does not qualify. Experience also arrives over time and from combat. A kill is useful, but spending the reward matters: open the shop during a live match to buy equipment you can afford.

      Six item slots hold equipment and consumables. Equipment bonuses apply while owned; an item key only activates items with an active effect. Potions use charges; an empty potion stays in its slot. Rebuying the same potion refills it for the full price. Drinking repeatedly refreshes its regeneration effect rather than stacking the rate. Buy anywhere while alive. The shop explains each item, its price and whether your inventory has room. There are no item-selling, discard or recipe controls; tier labels do not combine components. Permanent items cannot be duplicated.

      Damage strengthens attacks and abilities that scale with it; ability power strengthens spells that scale with it. Attack speed controls attacks per second; haste reduces ability cooldowns. Critical chance is the chance of a critical attack, and critical damage is its multiplier. Different kits reward different stats.

      The lane, Courts and victory

      Blue and red minions push the single lane. Your core sits at one end; the enemy core is at the other. Cores defend themselves, so push with minions instead of running at a healthy core alone. Right-click objectives to attack them: most damaging abilities do not hit Courts or cores.

      Return within your fountain near your own core to recover health and mana. If you die, wait for the displayed respawn countdown; you cannot move, cast or buy while dead. Your champion returns at its fountain with full health and mana. You retain gold, equipment and levels; temporary buffs end. There is no recall shortcut, so walk back to recover. The HUD shows team kills, both core health bars, boon timers, a minimap and your inventory.

      Destroy the enemy core to end the match. The result screen offers a return to the lobby. Online rooms are authoritative; if connection status reports a failure, return to the lobby and reconnect. Practice works without a match server after the game loads.
