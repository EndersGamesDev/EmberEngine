The story so far

Three chapters of the Crystalforge

The lane has a history. This is the part the five still argue about.

  1. 01

    The Forge Cools

    For a thousand years the forge burned at the world's centre, and the garden grew around it in rings of ivory and bronze. Then the smiths stopped coming, one by one, and the heat began its long walk outward. Two cores were left burning — one at each end of the old road — and the road was all that was ever built to carry what the fire could not.

    Five figures on the lane heard the first century of the cooling at once. They had been waiting for a reason. Now they had one.

  2. 02

    The Courts Wake

    At either shoulder of the lane stand the Courts, the two plazas the old smiths paved with the forge's first light. The North Court remembers force, and grants it to whoever holds it. The South Court remembers speed, and grants that too. Neither was built for a team. Both were built to be taken, and both wake the moment the fight becomes worth having.

    The first Court fell in the first hour, and the lane tilted — which was exactly what taking it was for.

  3. 03

    Five Reasons

    Every champion has a reason to be on the lane, and no two reasons fit the same hand. One is attention that became hunger. One is an oath that outlived its masters. One is a keeper who cannot say no. One is patience wearing stone. One is arithmetic with a deadline. The cores do not ask which reason is true.

    The winner does not own the forge. The winner relights it — and the lane will be walked again.

What you get on the lane

  • 1v1 or 3v3

    A private duel or a full squad, in online lobbies or against the bot brains — the same authoritative simulation either way.

  • Five distinct kits

    Drones or flame, healing light or bog iron, clockwork or storm — twenty abilities and five auto-attacks, each with its own language.

  • Draft with runes

    Pick your champion, then three runes from pages A, B or C. Your pages save, so your build is yours across matches.

  • Gold, items, potions

    Kills pay. The shop carries permanent stat items and drinkable potions across six slots, buyable anywhere on the lane.

  • The Courts

    The North Court grants damage; the South grants haste. Hold one and the lane tilts — lose it and it respawns for the other side.

  • Your controls, saved

    Every key remaps — abilities, spells, items, shop, stop — and your bindings persist in this browser.
