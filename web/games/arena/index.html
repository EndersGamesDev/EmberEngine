How it plays

Five hit points

A body takes five. A headshot ends it outright, so aim high — and crouching shrinks your own hitbox.

Slide and kick

Sprint, then press crouch while you are still moving, and you slide; jump out of it to carry the speed. In the air, hold crouch at a wall to slide it or kick off it — kicks chain between opposing walls, never twice off the same face.

Shield and blade

Q raises a Roman scutum that sends a bullet back at whoever fired it and detonates a rocket. E cuts with the Murasama, which kills outright and goes straight through a raised shield.

Three modes

Free for all to 20 frags, team deathmatch to 30, or king of the hill to 60. Every round ends, names a winner, and restarts with the scores reset and every block re-armed.

Your kit, until you die

Guns you pick up stay in slots 1–9 until you go down. A circular timer counts your reload, and LIFE and ammunition sit on the HUD where you can read them at a glance.

Classic or Custom

Classic starts everyone with the sidearm. Custom lets the host choose one starting weapon for the whole lobby, independently of the mode.

It runs in your browser.

No install, no account. WebGL2 and a keyboard, or a gamepad on Chromium.
