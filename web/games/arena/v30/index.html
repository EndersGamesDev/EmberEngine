Killshot

Find your route. Keep your momentum. Free for all, 4v4 team deathmatch or king of the hill across three hand-authored maps. Up to 8 players · 5 HP · respawn in 2.5 seconds.
New in v30 · protocol 23: a clean LIFE and AMMO LEFT display, a circular reload timer, ammo and health boxes, and a retained weapon inventory on 1–9. Play Classic or choose a shared starting weapon in Custom loadout. Your saved controls and Fullscreen mode remain in Settings.
Movement, controls & combat guide

Defaults: WASD move · mouse look · left click fire · right click aim · Space jump · Shift sprint · C crouch · R reload · E melee · Q shield · Tab scoreboard. Change any game action in Settings. Fullscreen has its own button; F11 is the browser shortcut.

Parkour: sprint, then hold crouch (C by default) for a ground slide. Jump during the slide to carry your speed. In the air, hold crouch against a wall to wall-slide. Release and freshly press jump (Space) to kick away; alternate between opposing walls to retain momentum and gain height. These moves use your current crouch, sprint and jump bindings.

Shield: hold for at most 3 seconds. You cannot fire while it is raised or for 0.35 seconds after lowering or exhaustion; reuse takes 5 seconds. Release the shield control before trying again. Bullets reflect; rockets explode on the shield. Melee cuts through it.

Weapons: Classic starts with the sidearm; Custom loadout gives everyone the host's selected weapon plus a backup sidearm. Jump into a floating ? block from below to collect a gun. Collected weapons stay in their numbered slots until you die, even when empty. Press 1–9 to switch and R to reload; all can be rebound in Settings. There are seven weapons now, with slots 8 and 9 reserved. Respawning restores your lobby's starting loadout.

Supplies: walk over an ammo box to refill finite ammunition or a health box to restore LIFE, up to 5. Used boxes return after 30 seconds. The sidearm has infinite reserve but still needs reloading. Duplicate weapon pickups do not refill ammunition. Each weapon has its own reload animation; the small circular timer shows time remaining. Headshots and melee are lethal. Walking is 4 m/s; crouching silences footsteps.

Rounds: free for all ends at 20 frags, team deathmatch at 30 team frags, and king of the hill at 60 points. Stand alone on the marked hill to score. After a 10-second round break everyone respawns and scores reset.

Maps: Breakwater Harbor adds three enterable service buildings, rooftop bridges, stairs and paired wall-jump lanes around its warehouse and quay. Ships and water remain scenery, not boarding or swimming routes. Freight Yard and Trench City offer tighter containers, rooftops and tunnels. All maps keep the same layout for everyone.

Gamepad: left stick move · right stick look · RT fire · LT aim · A jump · B crouch · LB shield · RB melee · X reload · L3 sprint · Start scoreboard. Settings remap keyboard and mouse; controller bindings remain unchanged.

Classic: everyone starts with a sidearm. Weapon pickups are kept until death. Loadout is independent of the match mode.
