Arena Shooter — free for all, team deathmatch, king of the hill

Click the game to capture your mouse (Esc releases) · WASD move · mouse look · click fire · Space jump · Shift sprint · C crouch · Q raises the scutum — a bullet comes back at the shooter, a rocket blows up on it · E cuts with the Murasama — kills outright and goes straight through a raised shield · headshots kill outright — aim above the chest · Tab scoreboard · RMB aims down the sights (the sniper scopes to a line) · R reload · F or ⛶ for fullscreen (Esc leaves it) · sound on 🔊 · 3 HP, respawn in 2.5 s · up to 8 players. Whoever creates a lobby is hosting; joiners drop straight in.
Three modes, picked when the lobby is created, and every one is a round that ends. Free for all is everyone against everyone and the first player to 20 frags wins the round. Team deathmatch splits the lobby into blue and red as you join, spawns each team on its own side, never lets a teammate's bullet, rocket or blade hurt you, and the first team to 30 frags wins. King of the hill makes the spot under the king block the hill (Freight Yard's loading dock, Trench City's statue plinth): standing on it alone earns a point every second, two or more on it earn nobody anything, and the first player to 60 points is king; frags still count and still show, but the hill is the score. When a round ends the winner is announced, the scoreboard comes up, and after a 10 s pause every score resets, everyone respawns with the sidearm, every ? block is re-armed and the next round starts.
Seven guns, and you get them by head-butting boxes. You spawn with the sidearm (infinite reserve). Everything better hangs in the air as a ? block: jump so your head hits it from below, Mario-style, and a random gun pops out — the Vityaz SMG that hoses a cone, the AK-47 that climbs, the revolver whose slug drops so you lead up, the sniper that goes through the first body it meets, the RPG-7 whose rocket blows up on anything with a 3 m splash you can hurt yourself with. Ammo is finite: one magazine plus one reserve, no refills, and when the last round is gone the sidearm is back. Death drops you to the sidearm too. A used block goes dark for 18 s. The blocks are the one reward on both maps: Trench City's four pads are gone, and a ? block hangs at the mouth of each of its tunnels instead.
Gamepad: a standard controller works beside the keyboard — left stick move, right stick look, RT fire, LT aim, A jump, B crouch, LB shield, RB melee, X reload, L3 sprint, Start scoreboard. Browsers surface a pad only after you press a button on it. Every gun has its own rumble on Chromium (Chrome, Edge); Firefox and Safari have no vibration API, so there the pad is input-only. The status line says which you got.
The map — Freight Yard. A rail freight yard at golden hour, the same for everyone, and the first map with a choice: whoever creates the lobby picks Freight Yard or v13's Trench City. Two trains of closed containers run east to west; nothing shoots through them and you cannot jump onto one from the floor. Crates and ammunition boxes are the steps up onto their roofs. Between the trains is the open yard with two 48 m lanes for the AK and the sniper, a loading dock you can jump onto and the king block hanging over it: from the floor it is a near-apex jump in the open, from the dock a hop. The train zone is boxed off from the yard and the backlots, a 48 m lane of its own with roofs for the SMG, the revolver and a lobbed rocket, with a ? block hanging off each crossing like a signal box, and two more over the middle containers that only the roof network reaches. Behind each train is a walled backlot where you spawn, leaving through two gaps. No pads here: seven blocks, and the fights are where the blocks are. Trench City plays the same game now: its four weapon pads are gone and four ? blocks hang at the tunnel mouths, one per side, in the open square just short of the inner wall, so you walk under one on the way into every tunnel and jump for it from the floor.
