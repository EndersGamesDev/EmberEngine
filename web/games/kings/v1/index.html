rules in short
  • The board. 10x10, four corners, one 4x4 block each: the corner 2x2 holds the legends (king, queen, hero, joker), the next ring the epics (two rooks, two bishops, a knight), the outer ring seven pawns. Seats move counter-clockwise; one action per 15 s turn; every action is a move of one piece.
  • King. One step in any direction. There is no check: a king may step into danger, and losing it eliminates you and removes all your pieces.
  • Queen, rook, bishop, knight. As in chess. Sliders stop at the first piece and capture it if it is not yours.
  • Pawn. Moves one tile forward or one tile left (the two outward directions from your corner: right and up on this board). Captures one tile diagonally on the three diagonals that are not backward. No double step, no en passant. A pawn reaching either far edge becomes a queen at once.
  • Joker. Steps one tile onto an empty tile, or teleports to any of its three mirror tiles (across the row, across the column, across the centre) if that tile is empty. On your own turns 5, 10, 15, ... it may instead be placed on any empty tile. It captures only the tile front-left of it in your seat's frame, wherever it stands and whichever way the board is turned; the small dot marks that tile. A joker on a far edge has no capture until it moves.
  • Hero. Starts dormant: it cannot move or capture, only be captured. Its one move is to swap with any of your pawns anywhere on the board: the pawn leaves the game, the hero takes its tile, and that is your whole turn. From then on it moves and captures like a rook and a knight combined. With no pawns left it may awaken where it stands instead.
  • Clock and elimination. A turn that runs out is a pass; three in a row eliminates you. A seat with no legal move passes instantly at no cost. Leaving the game eliminates you. Corners nobody sits in are garrisons: full formations that never move and that anyone may capture.
  • End. The last seated player with a king wins. 100 turns without a capture, a pawn move, a promotion or a hero swap, a full round of forced passes, or turn 600 end the game on material (queen 9, awake hero 8, rook 5, joker 4, knight 3, bishop 3, dormant hero 3, pawn 1); a shared maximum is a draw.
Rules, seat frames and the turn clock live in kings-core, the crate the server validates with and the client highlights with; the server is the only clock, and this page never counts past what it was told. The 3D view is a view only: the grid beside it is where you click.
