Arena Shooter — first-person drop-in deathmatch

Click the game to capture your mouse (Esc releases) · WASD move · mouse look · click fire · Space jump · Shift sprint · C crouch · Q shield — blocks the shot and sends it back at the shooter · E melee — kills outright and goes straight through a raised shield · headshots kill outright — aim above the chest · Tab scoreboard · RMB zoom · R reload · grab pads to upgrade your gun · jump onto the low crates and fight from up there · F or ⛶ for fullscreen (Esc leaves it) · a heavy revolver with a working cylinder, hammer and trigger, in real hands · sound on 🔊 · 3 HP, respawn in 2.5 s · up to 8 players. Whoever creates a lobby is hosting; joiners drop straight in.
The map — Trench City. A square in an old European city that is being fought over, the same for everyone. Closed shipping containers are hard cover: nothing shoots through them, and you cannot jump onto one from the floor. Wooden crates and ammunition boxes are the steps that get you up there — floor → ammo box → crate → container roof, and the roof is the high ground. A ring of trench lines circles the square, with fire steps against the outer wall: stand on one and you can see and shoot over it, stand on the floor of the trench and you cannot — and a hop from a fire step puts you on the wall top itself. Sections of the trench are covered tunnels — walk through underneath, fight on top, and no round passes through the roof. All four weapon pads are inside the tunnels, so the tunnels are what everyone is fighting over. A bronze statue on its plinth holds the centre of the square, and the city — cathedral, façades, street lamps, burnt-out cars — stands around it.
