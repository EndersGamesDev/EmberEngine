Touch: left thumb to move, drag the right side to look, tap Strike or Heavy, and hold Guard to defend. Tap Jump, then Heavy while airborne for a jumping slam. Press any controller button to connect. Vibration depends on your browser.

Three quick presses of left click, R1, or Strike connect the opening cuts. Aim at the head, either side of the torso, or either leg; the crosshair label shows your intended zone, and a brief hit label confirms contact. Each press adds one swing.

Use R, middle click, R2, or Heavy for a dedicated heavy attack. Jump first with Space, × Cross, or Jump for a downward airborne cut that holds its follow-through until landing. Quick taps: within 0.3 seconds. A deliberate pause: 0.3–0.9 seconds between taps. Your next swings are buffered. Holding attack does not repeat. Hold right mouse, F, L2, or Guard to raise the blade after committed swings finish. Face the incoming weapon. A knife block costs 28 stamina; castle weapons cost 22–38. An exhausted guard breaks. The Castellan’s crushing slam cannot be blocked: dodge sideways when he raises the maul. Release guard to recover stamina and strike during his recovery.
