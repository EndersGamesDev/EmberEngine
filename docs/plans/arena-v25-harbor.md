# Arena v25 — Breakwater Harbor

## Brief and ownership

The owner requested 4 m/s walking and a third, larger, realistic harbor map for eight players, based on real port references rather than randomly generated assets or layout. Work starts from main `1e2ec48` in the isolated `codex/harbor-v25` worktree. Existing Trench City and Freight Yard remain available; the shared checkout's uncommitted work is not overwritten. This release retains v24 weapon handling and the legacy horizontal air speed and jump arc. Existing stance multipliers make sprinting 6.4 m/s and crouch walking 2.2 m/s.

## Real references, original map

Google Maps satellite imagery was inspected at [the Maasvlakte terminal area](https://www.google.com/maps/@51.9554519,4.0152974,16z/data=!3m1!1e3): ships alongside straight quay faces, parallel crane rails, clear loading aprons, ordered container blocks and landside service roads. The [APM Terminals terminal description](https://www.apmterminals.com/en/maasvlakte/about/our-terminal) explains the separate waterside handling and onward transport areas, while its [yard overview](https://www.apmterminals.com/en/maasvlakte/modalities/yard) describes organized crane-served stacks. Container proportions follow the nominal external 20/40-foot dry-container dimensions in [Hapag-Lloyd's container specification](https://static-cf.hapag-lloyd.com/content/dam/website/downloads/local_info/HPL-CONTAINER_SPECIFICATION.pdf), not the smaller internal cargo dimensions. These references inform logistics and scale; this is a fictional compact combat terminal, not a reconstruction or simulation of APM's operations. No Google imagery, commercial branding or reference photographs are embedded in the game.

The ship, crane trusses, cables, spreaders, container corrugations, door bars, corner castings, markings and quay furniture are dimensionally authored geometry with fixed placements. Repeated ribs and truss members repeat a designed component; no random number generator determines the map or its scenery. Existing small material textures are reused within the engine's base-colour-only renderer. The map must not inherit Trench City's cathedral, cobbles or balustrade.

## Tactical plan

The playable square is 96 × 96 m (`arena_half = 48`), four times the area of each existing 48 × 48 m arena. Eight screened start positions are split four north and four south; team deathmatch uses those respective sides. Three route families run between them: the covered west service/warehouse flank, staggered central container storage and the east loading quay. Cross-links offer rotations between lanes; solid storage blocks interrupt direct spawn-to-spawn views. The quay offers longer engagements, container corners offer shorter encounters, and the central hill has multiple approaches. Rewards should draw players out of start pockets without requiring every player to cross one dominant sniper lane. The coordinate tables and executable invariants in `arena-core::harbor` are the source of truth; this plan does not duplicate a second mutable coordinate manifest.

Standard containers remain approximately 2.438 m wide and 2.591 m high with 6.06/12.19 m lengths. Warehouse and crane supports that can stop a player or bullet must be represented by the same shared obstacles the renderer draws. A raised warehouse roof keeps its bottom: movement, support, ceiling and bullet tests must agree. Steps and roof rewards must be tested using actual jump physics, not assumed reachable by eye. Water and moored ships sit outside the secured terminal boundary and are scenery; this release does not add swimming, boarding, sloping hull collision or a boat simulation.

## Bounds and protocol

The previous `Level.arena_half` was presentation metadata: `Sim::from_level`, horizontal collision, bullet boundaries and both server join paths still used the global 24 m constant. V25 carries per-level bounds through authoritative simulation, advertised `GameJoined`, initial prediction and reconciliation. The old public movement helpers retain their 24 m wrappers for existing callers; new bounded helpers explicitly receive the half-extent. Both existing maps must keep their old geometry and bounds. Invalid extents must not introduce NaN movement or unbounded worlds.

The eight-player team-spawn audit also found that global slots indexed a four-entry team list, aliasing teammates onto only two starts. Team-aware occupied-spawn selection must cover joining, leaving/rejoining, death and round reset. These changes and the 4 m/s speed require protocol 20 even though most frame shapes remain unchanged. Publish the matching server first, then Arena v25; archived v24 remains frozen and becomes list-only against the new exact join gate.

## Verification gates

Before release: native core/client/server tests and strict all-target Clippy; deterministic layout independent of seed; eight clear and pairwise-screened spawn positions; all required route endpoints connected for the actual player radius; measured route lengths/travel times; accessible loot and intended climbing chains; support, ceiling, bullet and large-boundary checks; initial and eight-player team round-reset uniqueness; old-map movement and ADS regressions. Browser tests render the actual release WASM at authored harbor viewpoints with eight synthetic players using only in-page events, never operating-system input. A separate real-server test uses a private eight-player lobby to verify metadata, actual positions, movement outside the old 24 m boundary, capacity, handling and protocol gating.

Rendering captures are visual evidence, not a frame-rate benchmark or proof of balanced competitive play. Automated movement and sightline gates do not substitute for eight humans playtesting the routes. The renderer still has a single world depth buffer, base-colour materials and sky/sun wet reflections rather than a modern PBR/scene-reflection pipeline. Static harbor water is an authored surface, not a fluid simulation. Record measured results and remaining gaps after execution rather than claiming them before the build.

## Executed verification — 2026-09-05

All builds, native gates and headless browser checks ran at Idle priority, with no operating-system input or visible game windows. The integrated native suite (`arena`, `arena-core`, `arena-server`, `ember-engine`, `ember-julibrot-app`, `what-is-this`, `fire`, `kings`, `ember-editor`) passed 695 tests, with four opt-in tests ignored, in 76.82 s. Final art additions were rechecked separately: all 122 Arena tests passed, and strict all-target Clippy passed for engine/Arena/core/server/Fire/Kings/editor (combined 10.94 s). Five release WASM bundles and bindings were rebuilt together; the final warm rebuild/binding step took 5.96 s. The Pages fixture passed 41/41 in 6.87 s and shell syntax passed 91/91 in 3.05 s.

Shared core tests prove all 56 directed spawn-to-spawn rays are blocked; a half-metre connectivity grid includes an additional 5 cm body-clearance margin. Actual fixed-step movement traverses the 74 m warehouse route in about 18.5 s, 69 m container route in about 17.25 s and 64 m quay route in about 16 s, with cross-links on both ends. Eight-player unique starts pass on all three maps/modes through join, leave/rejoin, individual/simultaneous respawn and round resets. A 900-tick Harbor replay matches independently replayed state; normal/sprint jump reaches remain 6.75/10.8 m.

The real protocol-20 local-server harness created separate password-protected eight-player FFA, TDM and Hill rooms, observed 2,578 states and three real shot events, required four players per TDM side, tested leave/rejoin and ninth-player rejection, rejected protocol 19 and moved beyond the old 24 m boundary. Measured walk/crouch/sprint speeds were 3.99994/2.20001/6.39999 m/s. All checks passed in 11.895 s; these were real sockets, not rendering fixtures.

The first browser captures exposed genuine PCF self-shadow stripes, independently reproduced with plain-texture GPU receivers and isolated by a browser-only no-shadow A/B. The receiver-plane fix is documented in `docs/environment-weather.md`: all eight previously failing receiver cases and the existing caster/cloud/wetness/particle checks now pass (two explicit GPU tests, 3.88 s wall time). Final normal-shadow WebGL2 captures use the actual release WASM, not the diagnostic replacement: 15 views at 1600×900, all 101 draw calls per checkpoint, no errors/warnings, 18.31 s, ANGLE on Intel UHD 770. The overview, player-level routes, ship/cranes, warehouse and all spawn views were visually inspected. The distant non-playable coast extends beyond the camera range; it does not enlarge collision bounds. Freight Yard and Trench City also joined and rendered with the new shader/protocol in 11.94 s; each reported one startup frame-stall warning (114/143 ms), so this is not a performance certification.

Final Arena bundle: 43,156,621 bytes, SHA-256 `21e2d8025b171d0e022a10479a8130903c4ef907ab69b8bba7c5ae5e4b85405d`. Reproducible harnesses are in `tools/v25`; machine-local reports/images are under ignored `target/harbor-network-local`, `target/harbor-release` and `target/harbor-old-maps`. Remaining limits include small PCF contact-edge bands at wall feet, base-colour-only low-detail materials, static off-map ships/water and the need for eight-human tactical playtesting; follow-ups are in `docs/plans/backlog.md`. The owner explicitly approved restarting the occupied live server after checks. Publish the matching server before the frozen v25 page and verify the public host and bundle afterward; no publication success is asserted by these pre-release gates.
