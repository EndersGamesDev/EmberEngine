# UltimateLegue V4 — crystalforge visual feedback and interface parity

The owner approved a focused V4 pass for UltimateLegue to improve map readability and feedback clarity without changing core rules. The scope is additive presentation and UI polish only, with protocol compatibility retained.

## Owner and integration model

This release is coordinated from the Codex seat (`codex/league-environment-feedback`) using V4 scene and client work prepared on shared branches. Fable owns environmental art and placement details, OpenCode owns read-only review of `web/games/league/v4` feedback behavior, and the release guard stays in Codex-owned `league` root wiring. The V4 work is designed to be a single branchable change with no cross-game process impact.

## V4 scope

- `scene.rs` changes from `e8631c96` rebuild the Crystalforge objective and lane silhouette: updated court rings, core silhouettes, objective geometry and non-trivial lane floor detail.
- `scene/art.rs` and `tools/league/art/bake_environment.py` changes from `39390fa5` replace the V4 ground, lane and court textures.
- `cb8a7198` and `0d4c374d` are the second ground and scene pass, made after the first integrated captures (below).
- `league-core` and `league-client` feedback contracts from `49a33c17` add session-scoped, monotonic feedback events with optional normalized screen coordinates, source/unit IDs and a compact `impact` envelope.
- UI iteration `70d4263f` updates the V4 overlay contract: target readout, ready/impact cues, unavailable action toast, reduced-motion fallback and gesture-gated sound cues.
- `a3e5e6ea` routes the launcher and landing path to V4 runtime files.
- `18f707eb` keeps the existing release guard in place so scoped V4 publishes do not overwrite unrelated game trees.

## Compatibility and constraints

No gameplay protocol bump is part of V4. Core movement, damage, cooldowns, wave timing and economy rules remain unchanged.

No new server features or network fields are introduced in this cycle. No host process or tunnel changes are part of this branch; this is a scene/UI pass in the existing V4-compatible runtime.

## Deliberately excluded from V4

- No combat formula, XP, or economy changes.
- No champion ability tuning or mechanics changes.
- No capture-time input automation.
- No launch story, trailer, or landing narrative changes.
- No protocol migration.

## The second ground and scene pass (fable, 2026-09-07)

Codex ran out of tokens after `70d4263f`; the owner handed the cycle to fable to finish with Spark (the Codex seat) and OpenCode, coordinated on Barza. The first integrated captures (`target/league-visual-v4`, root's 12:28Z run) showed three things the visual brief (Barza 227) had asked to avoid: the lane's 768 mirrored paving block at 14 × 1.4 tiles folded the large slabs into butterfly figures every 10 units; the garden's near-uniform olive picture read as a flat plane; and the objectives were small at mid-lane. What the captures also settled: nothing may stand above 16 cm inside the walkable rectangle (the scene test enforces it because the sim has no obstacles), so the brief's balustrade posts and reed band were dropped rather than faked.

`cb8a7198` re-bakes the two ground pictures without new picture jobs, except one: the accepted paving picture was offset by half in both axes and its cross seam regenerated by a single SDXL inpaint on specht (`SetLatentNoiseMask`, denoise 0.82, seed 6090742, job `fa485dea-c4f7-4126-a6e0-a8e43b46c65b`), then rolled back so the tile's edges are the picture's continuous centre. The lane ships as a plain 1024 repeat at 26 × 2.6 tiles with a warm ivory tint. The garden ships as a 768 mirrored block at 0.78 brightness with a jade tint, multiplied after the mirror by periodic low-frequency mottling (a random grid tiled on a torus, upsampled, centre-cropped), so it has patches a few champions wide, no seam and no mirror symmetry. `tools/league/art/bake_environment_pass2.py` reproduces both from the durable job records and carries the manifest forward; embedded art is 7,794,272 of 8,388,608 bytes.

`0d4c374d` cools the sun to (1.0, 0.90, 0.76) at 0.96 so the moss stays green, and grows the hearth obelisk from 0.60 to 0.72 (crystal at 4.15) and the Court obelisk from 0.40 to 0.46 (crystal at 2.85) with unchanged footprints; the pick-radius test still walks every vertex of both silhouettes.

Verified: `cargo test -p league --lib scene::` 10/10, native build, four hands-off captures at 72 s and 96 s (`tools/league/review/p2-*.png` in the fable worktree, `tools/league/review.ps1 -Mode squad -Cam <x,z[@zoom]>`), compared against root's first captures. Known remaining: a faint soft band recurs along the lane every 5.4 units where the seam was regenerated. A second inpaint at full denoise on a narrower band (`VAEEncodeForInpaint`, job `8e4d8b6a-bb53-402e-a333-5bd9db3aa004`) was tried and came out softer, not sharper, so it was not shipped; the fix, if wanted later, is a fresh seamless source rather than more inpainting. `rustfmt --check` reports pre-existing drift in `scene/art.rs` and `scene.rs` tests at `70d4263f`, not introduced by this pass. `cargo clippy --tests -D warnings` at `70d4263f` fails on 13 findings in `feedback.rs` (12 in library code, 1 in tests), assigned to Spark as a mechanical fix.
