# One dimension per job: genuine content in a compact world

Every construct introduced here is proposed. Names describe contracts rather than APIs or types.

## 1. Ruling: the internal dimension is double-booked

The 4D-first engine promises authoritative higher-dimensional solids, changing three-dimensional cross-sections, phase movement, and collisions outside the visible slice (`docs/4d-first-engine.md:5-7`, `docs/4d-first-engine.md:79-83`, `docs/4d-first-engine.md:120-122`). The compact torus world protects ordinary comprehension by placing intact 3D scenery instead of slicing it, then couples an actor's transverse support to the same near-period spectrum that governs recurrence and self-sight (`docs/4d-torus-world.md:23`, `docs/4d-torus-world.md:98-104`). Its published cubic example limits transverse half-support to `0.254 m` for self-sight beyond `500 m` and `0.0564 m` beyond `1 km` at `L = 128 m`, while its planning law makes image distance proportional to `L^(4/3) r_perp^(-1/3)` (`docs/4d-torus-world.md:108-121`).

That is one internal dimension doing two incompatible jobs. World topology needs small internal support to protect recurrence, injectivity, sightline, and self-image margins; genuine content needs meter-scale internal support so changing sections, extended interiors, and player phase have useful range. The adopted compact world resolves the collision by reserving sliced behavior for exceptional actors and making the environment intact and 3D, so its endless world succeeds by evicting most of the content that motivated 4D-first authority (`docs/4d-torus-world.md:23`, `docs/4d-torus-world.md:98-100`).

The ruling is one dimension per job. Ambient space is `R^5`, the quotient is a flat rank-5 torus, the physical leaf remains three-dimensional, and its two-dimensional internal complement separates a world circle from a content circle. Ordinary placed scenery remains intact and 3D; only genuine content actors and interiors occupy the content circle.

This ruling explicitly supersedes the adopted rejection of a 3D leaf in five spatial dimensions. That rejection correctly found that a better thickness exponent alone did not justify ten rotational planes, 2D windows, and 5D source assets; content occupancy was absent from that comparison, and once it is charged, the machinery becomes necessary while the previously computed exponent improvement becomes a windfall rather than the motive (`docs/4d-torus-world.md:108-119`).
