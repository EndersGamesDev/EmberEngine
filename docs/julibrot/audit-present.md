# Julibrot presentation audit

Audit baseline: `eb5ce23e956f2f96b557f3ae99a8cf9d0e867eba`; repository conclusions below are reviewed by reading that tree, native timings are the sokol measurements quoted in section 4, and browser-visible conclusions that those sources cannot establish are labelled unmeasured.

The presentation bar is continuous visible feedback under rapid input, no unintended clear-colour or partial-frame exposure, and reprojection that fits comfortably inside one refresh; the current pipeline is judged both as the shipped one-retained-view implementation and as the migration base described by `docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:1-23`.

## 2. Clear colour and partial-frame paths

Initial startup is an intentional whole-surface clear: `BrowserRuntime::clear_first_frame` acquires the surface, uses `LoadOp::Clear(clear_colour())`, submits, and immediately presents at `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/runtime.rs:295-334`; it is always reachable before the first scene, looks like the classic clear field, and the tile design still permits clear when no matching rendered content exists (`docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:232-236`).

The visible image-warp pass clears the acquired surface before drawing one fullscreen triangle at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/warp.rs:390-415`; an invalid source leaves the whole clear because the shader returns `clear_rgba`, while an admitted map leaves clear only where source UV is outside the retained image at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/warp_shader.rs:17-29`; fast pan, zoom-out, rotation, and translation can reach the local-border case, and the tile design substitutes multiple admitted depth-bearing candidates plus explicit counted holes (`docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:184-236`).

Incompatible slice or MAIN changes discard the retained scene at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device.rs:347-388` and `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/state.rs:140-165`, so object tilt, out-of-plane origin, cap, or precision can drive the next image-warp pass with no source and present a whole clear; the tile design also partitions genuine slice/cap/precision changes, but keeps every matching-content tile for pose-only movement and reserves whole clear for a partition with no matching rendered content (`docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:162-180,232-236`).

The main/backdrop scene pass clears an offscreen LDR scene texture to palette exterior-zero at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene.rs:235-275`; that clear is not presented directly, but pixels not covered by main or backdrop survive into the promoted texture and become visible through the following warp; fast relief controls can expose this honest hole, while the tile target distinguishes certified sky from unknown clear and schedules coarse coverage before refinement (`docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:210-236,247-274`).

The scene depth and stencil planes clear to far depth and empty backdrop ownership at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene.rs:277-291`; these are not colours, but their main-first/stencil-backdrop policy can preserve an uncovered colour hole, whereas the tile design uses one target-depth pass plus same-surface ownership before physical depth (`docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:202-229`).

The generic scene-mesh encoder clears its colour target at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene.rs:297-330`; `ReliefRedraw` now reaches it on the borrowed surface through `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/redraw.rs:43-58`, so backlog 326's former clear-only presentation is fixed, but redraw still shows clear wherever the one retained mesh does not rasterize; the tile replacement is multi-view full-chain mesh placement with target depth and coverage-first fallback.

The glitch census clears an offscreen count target to black at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/census.rs:45-68`; it cannot directly colour the canvas and is not a flash path.

## 4. Reprojection speed

The ordinary warp's CPU planner solves one four-corner homography, samples a 9 by 9 screen lattice at five relief heights, allocates a `Vec` for up to 405 errors, sorts it, and separately tests a 9 by 9 exposure lattice at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/planner.rs:247-303,338-380`; the native `profile.dev` (`opt-level=1`) x86 sokol probe measured 1,536 plans in 0.318604 s, or 0.207425 ms per plan, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=10.1s rootfs_tmp_left=242G workspace_left=242G`, while the release-profile native x86 probe measured 1,536 plans in 0.240761 s, or 0.156745 ms per plan, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=27.2s rootfs_tmp_left=242G workspace_left=242G`.

The ordinary GPU warp is one fullscreen triangle with no depth attachment, one retained-LDR `textureSample` for each covered fragment, one 288-byte HOT write, and one four-byte fence clear at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/warp_shader.rs:4-29`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/uniform.rs:8-14`, and `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/warp.rs:279-305,390-415`; at 1920 by 1080 the code-derived lower bound is 2,073,600 fragment texture samples, while actual fragments, GPU wall, bandwidth, and browser fence latency are unmeasured.

An exact `ReliefRedraw` is not the cheap fullscreen warp: it ensures canvas depth and a full scene index buffer, writes one 160-byte scene uniform, then draws the retained record mesh at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/redraw.rs:9-28,43-58` and `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene.rs:144-191`; the code-derived 1920 by 1080 mesh has 2,073,600 vertices and 12,423,606 `u32` indices, and its GPU wall is unmeasured.

The retained-view ledger alternates exactly two LDR texture indices and permits one pending scene at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/state.rs:56-90`, while the HOT ring has three 288-byte slots at `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device.rs:282-344` and `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/uniform.rs:8-14`; these are code contracts, not timings.
