# Julibrot compute audit

All source citations in this audit name the immutable baseline eb5ce23e; later commits are named only when establishing the disposition of an open line, and every timing identified as measured was collected on sokol through the required run-report wrapper.

Every size, count, bound, and policy constant described from a source citation is code-derived and not wall-measured unless its paragraph explicitly labels a sokol measurement; every browser or GPU wall without such a receipt is labelled unmeasured.

## 1. Latency ledger

The control-to-scene path is control mutation and navigation state, worker request construction, transfer into the worker, reference-orbit construction when deep precision requires it, credit-controlled response publication, response transfer and decoding, reference upload, refinement planning and heap dispatch, and scratch landing before presentation; the handoffs are visible at `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:542`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:281`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:59`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:115`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:111`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:267`, and `crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1053`.

Request encoding is fixed-cost metadata plus centre limbs proportional to precision: the wire layout is 116 bytes plus two little-endian limb vectors, each encode owns two `Vec<u8>` allocations, and decode owns two more vectors (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:16`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:44`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:97`).

The browser bridge allocates four maximum-size buffers at startup and a request uses only its compact prefix, while the native decode harness measured 675 us for 20,000 copies of a 628-byte prefix versus 14,290 us for copying the whole 32,832-byte buffer; this is 0.03375 us versus 0.7145 us per copy, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=0.2s` (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:132`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:308`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/wire.rs:13`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:1325`).

The reference centre is maintained at 1,024 bits and PictureFast navigation derives lower-width centres under a 10,000-edit error budget (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/big.rs:5`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/big.rs:22`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/big.rs:31`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:304`).

Reference-orbit construction is proportional to the iteration cap and currently grows each returned record vector by repeated pushes from an empty `Vec`; Deterministic always constructs a primary orbit at working precision and a verification orbit at 16 more decimal digits, while production PictureFast always takes the Preview pass and constructs only the primary (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:95`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:161`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:192`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:327`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:287`).

The shallow f32 path needs no reference construction or worker round trip: it accepts a reference-free configuration immediately, whereas the deep path submits a request and waits for an accepted orbit (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:281`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:297`).

The native shallow fixture measured 751.947279 ms before and 0.000100 ms after, saving 751.947179 ms, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=15.4s`, but its synthetic before arm builds a Deterministic primary-plus-verification orbit synchronously on the test thread rather than reproducing the former worker's 64-iteration cooperative chunks; PL-11's real user-side saving is one worker round trip plus its credit gate, which remains unmeasured in the browser (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/tests.rs:1319`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/tests.rs:1363`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:11`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:281`).

The harness's PictureFast Preview arm measured single-orbit generation at 307 us, a 4,096-byte payload, 6,534 ns mean packing, and 1,228 us deliberately depleted credit wait at cap 512; at cap 4,096 those values were 2,568 us, 32,768 bytes, 57,471 ns, and 10,272 us, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=11.2s` (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:305`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:540`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:239`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/credit.rs:153`).

The harness's Deterministic Final arm, which matches `PrecisionMode::default()`, measured the current default-mode paired-orbit generation cost as 634 us at cap 512 and 5,097 us at cap 4,096, with 2,536 us and 20,388 us depleted-credit waits; its 16-byte baseline record size determines the measured 8,192-byte and 65,536-byte payloads and 11,936 ns and 95,386 ns mean packing costs but does not affect generation, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=0.3s` (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/types.rs:69`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:95`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:327`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:305`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:550`).

Credit price is proportional to measured orbit-generation time but clamped to the interval from one microsecond to 250,000 us, and admission waits are individually bounded by one second (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/credit.rs:16`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/credit.rs:153`).

Orbit response packing is proportional to cap and writes eight indexed bytes per record into the JavaScript-owned array, so cap 4,096 performs 32,768 indexed setter calls; browser wall time for those calls is unmeasured (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:366`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:981`).

Request decode is bounded independently of maximum orbit capacity because only the used request prefix and trailer are copied before decoding, with the 628-byte measured upper case above (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:308`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:405`).

Preview, Interactive, and Final use scale divisors 4, 2, and 1 and absolute caps `requested_max_iter.min(64)`, `requested_max_iter.min(256)`, and `requested_max_iter.min(4_096)`; PictureFast applies one additional Preview scale divisor of 2 and skips Interactive (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:8`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:64`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:114`).

For a 960 by 540 deterministic view, code-derived rather than wall-measured work is Preview 240 by 135 at cap 64, or 2,073,600 pixel-iterations and 518,400 output bytes; Interactive 480 by 270 at cap 256, or 33,177,600 pixel-iterations and 2,073,600 bytes; and Final 960 by 540 at cap 4,096, or 2,123,366,400 pixel-iterations and 8,294,400 bytes (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:236`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:261`).

For the same view, PictureFast Preview is code-derived as 120 by 68 at cap 32, or 261,120 pixel-iterations and 130,560 bytes, followed directly by the same Final workload; GPU execution wall time for every level is unmeasured and requires the browser (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:64`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:261`).

The native planning harness measured 10,000 real `harness.plan(index)` calls at 5,815 us, 4,538 us, and 9,786 us for Preview, Interactive, and Final, while its reported 3 us after arm merely black-boxes a cached-template reference, a roughly 0.3 ns pointer read per iteration rather than a plan; `before_allocations_at_least=5` and `after_allocations=0` are hardcoded format-string literals rather than allocator measurements, so this harness establishes neither an end-to-end speedup nor measured allocation counts, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=0.3s` (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:940`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:951`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:236`).

Each level reserves one whole 8,388,608-byte final-size output span even when it writes only a Preview or Interactive prefix, and a paired grid reserves 16,777,216 bytes; these are code-derived capacities, not wall measurements (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:145`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:224`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:261`).

At 960 by 540, page splitting is code-derived as one dispatch page and two copy commands for Preview, two pages and three copy commands for Interactive, and eight pages and eight copy commands for Final (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:261`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:681`).

Each page resolves and compares the entire static header block before encoding, so the 2,048-byte Julibrot header is compared eight times during the code-derived Final plan; header reserve upload occurs only on a difference (`crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:802`, `crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:957`).

Descriptor-table and span-directory synchronization each call a packing routine that allocates a fresh word vector when metadata is dirty; the app config makes their code-derived uploads 1,024 bytes and 768 bytes respectively, and browser wall time is unmeasured (`crates/labs/heap/src/heap.rs:485`, `crates/labs/heap/src/span.rs:287`, `crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1009`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:418`).

Every encoded page allocates a texture-view vector and an optional-attachment vector and creates a scratch texture view, making the code-derived Final overhead 16 heap allocations and eight scratch views before command submission; browser wall time is unmeasured (`crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1053`, `crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1155`).

Scratch landing copies the logical row range into a 512 by 512 by four-layer, 16-byte-pixel scratch texture whose configured capacity is code-derived as 16,777,216 bytes; copy count scales with page count, while GPU copy and fence wall times are unmeasured (`crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1053`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:418`).

## 2. Fast-slide behavior

Zoom, pan, origin, centre, iteration-cap, and precision changes navigate and release the current reference; object-angle edits navigate only when they change the active plane, while an in-plane reorientation does not; palette and view-only changes do not navigate (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:542`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:577`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:796`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:866`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:926`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:952`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:977`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:1000`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:1015`).

The owner admits at most one active request and retains only the latest pending request, so an arbitrarily long burst produces one in-flight request plus one coalesced successor rather than an unbounded request queue (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:372`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:461`).

The worker-side pending slot is also latest-wins, the arrival endpoint has two FIFO slots, and the application drains at most those two arrivals on a service pass; the four-buffer pool therefore bounds owned transfer memory (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:59`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:261`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:318`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:536`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:583`).

Generation checks occur before each orbit chunk, the chunk is at most 64 iterations, and the elapsed-time check runs every eight iterations against a 2,000 us target, so ordinary stale work yields cooperatively; the browser distribution of individual high-precision steps and actual yield intervals is unmeasured (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:11`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:148`).

Cancelled or stale responses are still published or drained so their buffers and credits return, and the application accepts only the exact current generation and pending identity before upload (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:738`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:627`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:149`).

Worker yielding uses a zero-delay timer, credit admission expires in at most one second per attempt, resize drains expire after the code-defined 4,000,000 us deadline, and application service uses nonblocking pumps; a closing drain is unguarded because `advance_drain` returns on `resize: None` before its deadline check, so the never-hang law is not met if an acknowledgement or slot never returns, while browser event-loop and device-completion behavior remain unmeasured (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:964`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/credit.rs:153`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/channel.rs:24`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:449`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:468`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:477`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:595`).

An iteration-cap change on a deep view arms a four-slot resize drain before submission, and `pump_request` refuses every request while any drain exists; the newest orbit therefore remains on the main thread until all four slots and `ShutdownAck` return, or until the code-defined four-second resize deadline raises `BufferStarved`, with browser incidence unmeasured (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:244`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:449`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:596`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/channel.rs:24`).

Deep-path reference currency requires bit-exact zoom equality, so even a one-ULP zoom change invalidates an otherwise compatible centre-and-plane reference and keeps `scene_ready` false until orbit generation, credit admission, transfer, expansion, and span upload complete; one such round trip per bit-distinct slider value is code-derived, while its browser wall is unmeasured (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:350`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:111`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:169`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:347`).

FIFO draining and exact-generation acceptance prevent an older orbit from being installed after a newer orbit by code review, but a fast navigation resets census progress and no candidate accompanies the new navigation, so repeated motion can indefinitely postpone secondary glitch correction without hanging the main request path (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:234`, `docs/plans/backlog.md:332`).

The native queue harness drained 20,000,000 synthetic arrivals in 81,690 us with shifting-vector removal and 42,863 us with a fixed queue, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=0.4s`; current production uses the fixed bounded endpoint storage, so this result confirms the bounded shape rather than identifying a current unbounded backlog (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:261`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:1252`).

A 10,000-edit native navigation harness at zoom 100 and width 1,024 measured 4,783.333 ms for deterministic recomputation and 4,644.672 ms for PictureFast, saving 138.661 ms while accumulating 0.001557775202 pixel final error, or 0.0000001557775202 pixel per edit, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=9.6s` (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/big.rs:22`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:304`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:790`).

## 4. Correctness under speed

The CPU oracle propagates an explicit f64 error envelope through perturbation, rescale, and rebase, whereas production WGSL evaluates the scaled perturbation and the Pauldelbrot single-step glitch predicate without carrying that envelope (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/perturb.rs:84`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/perturb.rs:160`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.wgsl:191`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.wgsl:247`).

The boundary harness measured maximum norm error 0.000015258788835 against an envelope of 0.000045776369689, a tightness ratio of 3.0000002086, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=0.2s`; the mixed rescale-and-rebase harness measured four cases, zero boundary samples, and zero violations with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=0.3s` (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/perturb.rs:84`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.rs:719`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.rs:781`).

The WGSL rebase switches the perturbation origin to the current reference sample when the single-step condition fires, and records numeric or exhausted-reference glitches as distinct negative codes; a changed main reference cannot splice into an already dispatched grid because generation and accepted-reference guards reject mismatches (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.wgsl:247`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.wgsl:254`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.rs:254`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:476`).

The f32/deep switch is explicit: the shallow configuration carries no orbit, while deep configuration is blocked until the exact generation and precision policy are accepted (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:281`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:297`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:149`).

The ABI harness measured 16 fixtures, 20,464 records, 40,928 coordinates, and zero changes to consumed high words with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=2.6s`, supporting the current low-word WGSL consumption rather than proving future high-word use (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:619`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.wgsl:128`).

The accumulated-error sweep measured 518,400 samples: at threshold 0.0001 it flagged 3,871, including 2,547 wrong escapes, 408 verified-correct escapes, and 916 no-escape cases; at 0.001 it flagged 471, including 347 wrong, two verified-correct, and 122 no-escape cases; at 0.01 it flagged 58, including 23 wrong and 35 no-escape cases, with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=33.5s` (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/conformance.rs:477`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.rs:298`).

At threshold 0.001 the corrected-Final harness flagged 3,071 samples with 2,140 old-result mismatches, 926 matches, and five glitches, while 57 of 1,024 sampled unflagged points were wrong; the measured true-positive fraction was 0.697977821 and sampled false-negative fraction 0.055664062, so this detector is not yet a safe value-preserving production change (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/conformance.rs:628`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.rs:298`, measurement `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=33.5s`).

The accumulated-cost harness measured 2,115,025 us for the baseline detector and 2,075,724 us for accumulated detection over 100,000 rounds with 27 rebases per round, a noisy negative delta of 14.556 ns per rebase, while the separate Pauldelbrot harness measured 3,456,316 us for 102.4 million iterations, or 33,753 ps per iteration; browser GPU cost is unmeasured (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/conformance.rs:844`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/conformance.rs:898`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.rs:298`, `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=33.5s`).

PictureFast is an explicit policy mode rather than a deterministic receipt: `PrecisionMode` defaults to Deterministic, while the app explicitly opts its initial controls into PictureFast (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/types.rs:69`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:65`).

Every production PictureFast request is hardwired to `ReferencePass::Preview`: the baseline source search found the only `with_precision_policy` calls in a test-only measurement path and a codec test, while application submission calls `OrbitRequest::new` without it, making verification false and the higher-precision restart unreachable for PictureFast; under the owner ruling this is the fast tier doing its intended job, not a defect by itself (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:287`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:399`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:673`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:303`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:95`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:161`).

PictureFast working precision is only coordinate digits plus eight guard digits plus iteration digits and is never verified or escalated, so a deep reference that Deterministic would rebuild at 16 more digits can ship inaccurate pixels; that is allowed while moving and creates the need for an eventual static deterministic replacement (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/scale.rs:145`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:161`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:287`).

The present facts expose only the honest precision-mode name, while application acceptance discards `reference_verification`, maximum consumed-word error, and precision escalation facts whose response getters have no non-test consumers; nothing falsely labels PictureFast as Deterministic, but the page cannot disclose that the orbit is unverified or quantify its consumed-word error (`crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device.rs:350`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:182`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/channel.rs:723`).

No static deterministic swap exists: PictureFast advances from Preview to Final and then stops, and the baseline non-test scheduling path contains no promotion from PictureFast to Deterministic, so a settled PictureFast view retains the fast result indefinitely (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:58`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/schedule.rs:680`).

Three native Final conformance tests reported green with `RUN-REPORT exit=0 wall=0.4s`, but the shared shallow boundary arm returns the same value for both modes and all five fast helpers for rendering, special values, category colour, clear colour, and warp rows delegate to their deterministic counterparts; every exact-versus-fast assertion therefore compares the same implementation, proving no current PictureFast divergence and leaving prospective test debt rather than evidence of a current wrong pixel (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:70`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:361`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:384`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:489`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:582`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:597`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:608`).

Exact generation checks at arrival, GPU reference acceptance, scene readiness, and the view stamp prevent a completed grid for an old plane, reference, centre revision, cap, or precision mode from being submitted as the current scene by code review; these identity guards do not detect accumulated numerical misclassification, and the complete rapid-slider browser sequence remains unmeasured (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:149`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:476`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:347`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:446`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.rs:298`).

## 5. Tile readiness

The tile design requires per-tile source-screen regions as jobs, one shared and pinned reference orbit per MAIN generation, paired value and reconstruction records, transactional refinement in which value and depth complete together, a demand-ordered queue, and bounded resident budgets (`docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:71`, `docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:141`, `docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:227`, `docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:233`, `docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:241`, `docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:249`).

Useful foundations already exist in the heap's bounded descriptor and span directories, paired contiguous span allocation, logical-page dispatch, explicit reference generation identity, and latest-wins worker admission (`crates/labs/heap/src/heap.rs:485`, `crates/labs/heap/src/span.rs:287`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:224`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:681`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:372`).

The present compute shape is wrong for tiles because a refinement owns a whole-screen final-size reservation, the kernel emits one value record rather than a value-and-reconstruction pair, refinement is a serial global ladder, worker admission describes reference-orbit requests rather than tile jobs, and the two-entry reference-template cache is not a pinned generation lease (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:145`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:114`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:16`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:606`).

The smallest stage-0 foundation is a wire-free, value-preserving job model in math and kernels containing content, MAIN, and reference identities, a source-screen rectangle, refinement rung, demand key, paired output-span plan, and a reference lease, plus pure validation and budget arithmetic; this matches the design's policy-and-math stage without changing request layouts, kernels, or delivered values (`docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:227`, `docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:349`).

That foundation is estimated at two to three engineering days with low runtime risk and medium API-shape risk; implementation is intentionally absent from this audit because the tile design belongs to the next work (`docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:349`).

## 6. Ranked findings and recommendations

The owner-ruled rendering ladder is reprojection, fast-and-ugly, fast, then Deterministic for explicitly selected Deterministic mode or as the eventual replacement for a static fast view; a moving frame may be very inaccurate but must remain honest about its tier and must not assert false set content.

The ranking criterion is, in order, a frame that is wrong through false content, label, or receipt; a user wait or frozen slide; an honestly labelled but inaccurate moving frame or missing quality rung; and CPU or allocation cost, with prospective test debt last inside the fourth class.

### Critical — wrong content or receipt honesty

- C1 — Accumulated cancellation error can ship false set content (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.rs:298`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.wgsl:247`): a deep point can return a wrong escape without tripping the single-step predicate, so the moving frame asserts a false fact about set membership rather than merely lowering quality; the fix shape is a conservative oracle-tuned detector followed by bounded regional secondary-reference correction, with high numerical and performance risk, direct tile-prep relevance, and an estimate of three to five days after detector tuning.

- C2 — Verification facts are computed but not published (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/channel.rs:723`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:182`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device.rs:350`): a PictureFast frame is honestly named PictureFast but its consumer cannot distinguish Deferred from Stable verification or report consumed-word error and escalations, leaving a receipt honesty gap; the fix shape is publishing the existing verification enum and error fields through app and presentation facts without forcing PictureFast verification, with low data risk and medium cross-package contract risk, tile-prep relevance to per-generation receipts, and an estimate of one day in the app/presentation lane.

- C3 — Backdrop cache identity omits delivered extent (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/backdrop.rs:64`): a changed heap derivation under the same requested extent, cap, and mode can reuse an incompatible backdrop and display stale content as current; the fix shape is including delivered extent and allocation identity in the cache key, with low correctness risk, tile-prep relevance to tile identity, and an estimate of half a day outside this lane's writable scope.

### High — waits or freezes

- H1 — Deep reference currency is keyed to bit-exact zoom (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:350`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:347`): one ULP of zoom on a deep slide invalidates a reference with compatible centre and plane, blocks refinement, and waits for generation, credit admission, transfer, expansion, and upload for every bit-distinct value; the fix shape is an app-owned compatible-reference lease keyed by plane, centre, precision, and sufficient orbit length while scale and pixel offsets remain per-job, with medium numerical-compatibility risk, direct tile-prep relevance, and an estimate of one to two days outside this lane's writable scope.

- H2 — Iteration-cap changes block request transfer behind a four-slot resize drain (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:244`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:596`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/channel.rs:24`): dragging the cap in a deep view leaves the latest orbit request on the main thread until all slots and `ShutdownAck` return, with a code-defined worst case of four seconds followed by `BufferStarved`; the fix shape is retaining a stable maximum-cap pool or debouncing coalesced cap edits into one resize while preserving latest-generation admission, with medium memory and protocol risk, strong tile-prep relevance to bounded admission, and an estimate of two days plus browser replay.

- H3 — Closing drains have no deadline (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:449`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:468`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:477`): shutdown after a lost acknowledgement or slot leaves `shutdown_acknowledged` false without a terminal transition, violating the never-hang law; the fix shape is applying a closing deadline before the `resize: None` return and recording a typed terminal reconciliation outcome, with medium ownership-risk, tile-prep relevance to bounded resource leases, and an estimate of one day.

- H4 — Orbit transfer uses per-byte JavaScript setters (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:366`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:981`): cap 4,096 performs 32,768 setter calls per orbit, so rapid deep changes can make a ready reference arrive late and prolong retained reprojection; the fix shape is a validated bulk typed-array copy preserving exact wire bytes, with medium browser interoperability risk, tile-prep relevance for shared-reference delivery, and an estimate of one day plus browser measurements.

- H5 — Request return performs maximum-capacity clearing and validation (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:228`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:428`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/wire.rs:676`): every small request returned from a cap-4,096 buffer can clear and scan roughly 32 KiB during a burst, adding worker-main-thread pressure and slider lag; the fix shape is message-kind-aware used-range clearing and validation with retained-tail invariants, with medium stale-byte safety risk, tile-prep relevance to bounded queues, and an estimate of one to two days plus browser tests.

- H6 — Reference upload expands into a square heap page (`crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:726`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:169`): a cap-4,096 orbit has 65,536 logical expanded bytes but allocates and uploads a 1,048,576-byte 256-square staging vector, a code-derived 16-fold amplification that can delay every accepted deep reference; the fix shape is a row-exact or padded-row upload path proven against WebGPU layout constraints, with medium backend risk, strong tile-prep relevance, and an estimate of two days including browser validation.

- H7 — Credit safety can add visible delay (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/credit.rs:153`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/compute.rs:540`): a depleted balance made the measured cap-4,096 PictureFast request wait 10,272 us, and stale work can defer the newest request even though each admission attempt is bounded; the fix shape is generation-aware refund and admission accounting without bypassing forward pressure, with medium fairness risk, tile-prep relevance for demand ordering, and an estimate of one to two days.

- H8 — Retired transient grids remain representable (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/submit.rs:260`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/submit.rs:294`): an allocation failure during replacement leaves a retired span named by state and permanently stops refresh through a typed error, showing a frozen scene rather than false content; the fix shape is atomically forgetting presentation state before freeing the grid, with low risk, tile-prep relevance to transactional retirement, and an estimate of half a day outside this lane's writable scope.

- H9 — PictureFast moving Final retains the full requested cap (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:64`): a deep moving view at cap 4,096 still schedules the full Final workload, making the user wait for accuracy that the moving-tier policy does not require; the fix shape is an honestly receipted motion-aware cap rung followed by the static deterministic promotion, with high policy and visual-transition risk, direct tile-prep relevance to rung planning, and an estimate of two to four days plus browser validation.

### Medium — inaccurate frames or missing quality rungs

- M1 — A settled PictureFast view never swaps to Deterministic (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/refinement.rs:58`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/schedule.rs:680`): after motion stops, PictureFast Final becomes terminal and the lower working-precision result remains indefinitely instead of being replaced by the owner's deterministic static rung; the fix shape is an app-side stillness-triggered Deterministic request and transactional scene swap that never blocks moving tiers, with high scheduling and memory risk, direct tile-prep relevance to transactional refinement, and an estimate of two to three days outside this lane's writable scope.

- M2 — Census evidence is discarded across navigation (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:234`, `docs/plans/backlog.md:332`): repeated compatible nudges reset candidate progress, so an honestly flagged residual glitch can remain visible throughout motion even after the prior view found a useful secondary reference; the fix shape is reprojecting and carrying a compatible candidate observation into the next MAIN generation, with high plane-compatibility and ownership risk, direct tile-prep relevance to pinned reference leases, and an estimate of two to three days outside this lane's writable scope.

### Low — CPU, allocation, observability, or prospective debt

- L1 — Several walls are invisible (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/timing.rs:92`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/timing.rs:114`): a two-second slider hold can miss transfer, upload, dispatch, or fence delay and report no causal stage, leaving a wait unactionable without itself causing the wait; the fix shape is a browser receipt with monotonic marks across every handoff, with low runtime risk, tile-prep relevance for per-tile service budgets, and an estimate of one to two days.

- L2 — Final dispatch repeats whole-header comparisons (`crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:957`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:681`): an eight-page Final compares the same 2,048-byte header eight times, a code-derived count independent of the null planning benchmark arm; the fix shape is plan-level comparison and one dirty decision, with low correctness risk, tile-prep relevance for many small jobs, and an estimate of one day.

- L3 — Metadata synchronization allocates packed vectors (`crates/labs/heap/src/heap.rs:485`, `crates/labs/heap/src/span.rs:287`, `crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1009`): descriptor or span dirtiness allocates and repacks before dispatch, producing allocator pressure as resources churn; the fix shape is reusable packing scratch or direct encoding, with low-to-medium lifetime risk, strong tile-prep relevance, and an estimate of one to two days.

- L4 — Page encoding allocates transient collections and scratch views (`crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1053`, `crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1155`): an eight-page Final creates the code-derived 16 small vectors and eight views, a source count independent of the hardcoded allocation literals in the planning harness; the fix shape is retained page scratch with bounded capacities, with medium borrow and lifetime risk, strong tile-prep relevance, and an estimate of two days.

- L5 — Reference construction grows record vectors incrementally (`crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:192`, `crates/labs/julibrot/math/src/orbit.rs:239`): cap-4,096 construction can reallocate while the user waits, and Deterministic doubles the vector count for verification; the fix shape is exact preallocation for the known cap with a native allocation pin, with low risk, shared-reference tile-prep relevance, and an estimate of half a day.

- L6 — Final conformance is universal rather than comparative (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:361`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:384`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:489`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:582`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:597`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:608`): one shared shallow arm and five deterministic-delegating fast helpers make every exact-versus-fast assertion compare one implementation with itself, but no current divergent PictureFast kernel exists to produce a present wrong pixel; the fix shape is five independent fast helpers plus a mode-distinct shallow fixture tied to production policy, with low runtime risk and medium oracle risk, no direct tile-prep role, and an estimate of one to two days outside this lane's writable scope.

- L7 — Plane-axis constants remain request data (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:26`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/codec.rs:16`): a future axis-contract mismatch could compute a different plane than the caller expects, although no current mismatch is evidenced and generation guards cannot detect semantic disagreement; the fix shape is a versioned immutable axis contract in a future wire revision, with medium compatibility risk, tile-prep relevance to job identity, and an estimate of one day after the layout freeze.

- L8 — Bundle determinism is unresolved (`docs/plans/backlog.md:260`): a toolchain-dependent bundle could alter browser behavior without a source diff, but this lane cannot reproduce the build by rule; the fix shape is a separately authorized reproducible bundle check, with low source risk, no tile-prep role, and an estimate of one day in the browser lane.

### Recommendations

1. Conservative accumulated-error detection and bounded secondary-reference correction form the first recommended lane because the current miss can publish false set content (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.rs:298`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/perturb.wgsl:247`).

2. Publication of Deferred or Stable verification and consumed-word error, followed by an app-owned settled Deterministic promotion, forms the second recommended cross-package lane without slowing PictureFast motion (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/channel.rs:723`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/schedule.rs:680`).

3. Compatible deep-reference reuse and stable-pool or debounced cap admission form the third recommended lane because they remove whole round trips from continuous sliders (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:350`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:244`).

4. A bounded terminal state for closing drains forms the fourth recommended lane because the current `resize: None` path violates the never-hang law (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:468`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:477`).

5. Browser wall capture for an exact 2,000 ms hold and a rapid-change burst, followed by byte-identical bulk orbit packing and used-range request returns, forms the fifth recommended lane (`docs/plans/backlog.md:258`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:228`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/browser.rs:981`).

6. A wire-free stage-0 tile job, paired-output plan, compatible reference lease, ordering key, and budget arithmetic form the sixth recommended lane, preceding any tiled dispatch or value change (`docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:227`, `docs/julibrot/tiled-reprojection.md:349`).

7. Removal of heap-side upload and dispatch amplification is the seventh recommended lane, ordered as reference staging, per-page header comparison, metadata packing allocations, and page scratch allocations (`crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:726`, `crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:957`, `crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1009`, `crates/labs/heap/src/executor.rs:1053`).

8. Independent fast conformance helpers and compatible census carry form the eighth recommended validation-and-quality lane after current false-content and wait defects are bounded (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/tests/final_conformance.rs:384`, `docs/plans/backlog.md:332`).
