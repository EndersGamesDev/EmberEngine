### Ownership quality against the slice contracts

**Verified.** `ViewerController` is the principal mixed owner: its fields combine app-requested intent and revision, math-produced plane/map/footprint caches, a worker-owned `ViewerOwner`, shadow HOT/MAIN records, reference-request state, navigation mirrors, and crosshair/UI state; its edit methods invoke worker navigation directly and its drains also construct the present-facing `Pose`. This is legal integration under the app contract, but it collapses J-01, J-03, and the math-to-present adapter into one type rather than exposing ports between them (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:397-435`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:563-625`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:1264-1328`).

**Verified.** `BrowserFrameLoop` is the second mixed owner: one struct holds device and queue, heap executor, kernels, presenter, worker endpoints, orbit registry and accepted leases, current and spare grids, backdrop state, refinement schedule, surface-facing capture, event/facts state, and timing. App is supposed to integrate these slices, but this storage shape makes all lower boundaries fields of one browser-only coordinator and prevents the individual J-02, J-07, J-10, and J-12 transactions from being replayed through narrow ports (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:672-772`).

**Verified.** App reaches through `ViewerController` into worker internals through public `owner()` and `owner_mut()`: scheduling mutates precision policy, reference handling accepts orbits and navigation, submit guards query pending depth, and saved-view capture reads the authoritative centre. Those callers bypass the controller's otherwise app-facing transaction surface and obstruct a replaceable J-03 owner/drain port (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:548-557`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/schedule.rs:833-846`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:180-230`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/submit.rs:107-118`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/saved.rs:170-180`).

**Verified.** Worker has two owner-channel implementations rather than two lowerings of one implementation: `ChannelCore` keeps queues, pending request, generation/revision, credit, facts, leases, resize, and pump logic for same-thread use, while generic `OwnerCore` repeats those fields and the submit/return/resize/facts transitions for the browser transport. The endpoint header claims both native and browser drive the identical state machine, but `WorkerChannel::new` selects `ChannelCore` for same-thread and `OwnerCore` only through `BrowserOwnerEndpoint`; this is a contract-quality defect at J-02 because equivalence depends on tests rather than construction (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/channel.rs:60-136`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/channel.rs:901-1098`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:1-10`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:158-188`).

**Verified.** Tile vocabulary is split across slices: kernels and present both define `TileResidency` and `TileQuality` with different fields and ordering, kernels alone defines signed `SourceScreenRect`, and present alone defines unsigned `SourcePixelRect`, `TileRung`, `DescriptorSamplePair`, and canonical chart ownership. The shipped app, worker, whole-grid kernels, planner, and device path do not call these stage-0 types; their only scoped callers are their defining modules' tests, so this is deliberately dormant scaffolding rather than a live J-06/J-08 port (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/tile_job.rs:141-172`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/tile_job.rs:345-376`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/tile.rs:90-110`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/tile.rs:368-443`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/tile_job.rs:708-980`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/tile.rs:555-861`).

**Derived.** The whole-grid path otherwise respects the written slice direction: kernels own the `EscapeGrid` and its heap lifetime, present borrows/clones it into `PresentMain` and retains completed `SceneFrame` identities, and app owns surface acquisition through terminal presentation. The refactor should preserve those ownership facts while making the intervening ports explicit rather than moving policy down-slice.

### Test-harness sprawl and duplicated fixtures

**Verified.** The app frame-loop harness defines `FakePresenter`, `FakeClock`, `drive_turn`, `drive_refresh`, and `drive_viewer_harness`, then adds a separate `ZoomScript`/`ZoomTurnRecord` driver and a separate `HeightDragRow`/`HeightDragStats` driver. All three reconstruct scene identities, refinement progress, warp kind, retained source, and presentation turns, so they duplicate the same fixture vocabulary without producing one comparable frame trace (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/tests.rs:685-1124`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/tests.rs:2402-2927`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/tests.rs:3148-3406`).

**Verified.** Present repeats pose/frame fixtures at three levels: planner's in-file `frame` and pose builders exercise pure plans, device tests define `binding_pose`, `binding_main`, `frame_on`, and `pose_on` for ledger/GPU-shape assertions, and the native `reprojection_oracle`, `steep_row`, and `steep_frame` suites independently rebuild the same source/destination pose families. The layers test different contracts, but the pose and `SceneFrame` constructors are duplicated and make a single planner-result corpus difficult to compare (`crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/planner.rs:1001-1035`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/planner.rs:1610-1648`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/tests.rs:178-257`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/tests.rs:1090-1107`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/tests.rs:1323-1344`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/tests/reprojection_oracle.rs:1`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/tests/steep_row.rs:1`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/tests/steep_frame.rs:1`).

**Verified.** Worker has a small tuple-based `trace` over public `WorkerChannel` and a much larger `FakeSlot`/`FakePort`/`Harness` around `OwnerCore`; because same-thread public construction bypasses `OwnerCore`, those harnesses prove parallel implementations rather than replaying identical events through two ports (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/channel.rs:1415-1460`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:732-1034`).

**Derived.** Moving fixture constructors into small test-support modules is useful only after each oracle has one owned trace/result record. Mere file splitting would reduce line length but would not remove duplicated semantic fixtures or protect a seam.

**Verified.** Concrete producers are `SavedView::capture`/`lerp` and the `ViewerController` setters for zoom, pan, object/origin, centre, iteration cap, precision, palette, VIEW, preset, and a sampled reference. `ViewerController` owns the requested record, a saturating `u64 requested_revision`, shadow HOT/MAIN records, and the concrete `ViewerOwner`; navigation creates `u32` generations and centre revisions inside the owner. Edits allocate only where bignum centres or saved `Vec` limbs are cloned/encoded, write `Cell`-backed staged HOT/MAIN and app fields, and cross no GPU fence (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/saved.rs:26-99`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/saved.rs:170-280`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:411-435`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:530-840`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:1163-1262`).

**Proposed.** Make J-01 a real port by introducing one app-owned `ViewerEdit` plus `ViewerEditBatch { requested_revision, edit }` return from setters and one adapter method that applies a batch to J-03; keep `RequestedControls` and `SavedView` app-specific and do not put Julibrot fields into a graph core. This smallest change separates intent from owner mutation while retaining the exact setter behavior and revision sequence.

### J-02 — ownership-transfer seam

**Verified.** `BrowserFrameLoop::new` calls `WorkerChannel::new`, then `submit_pending_reference` sends `OrbitRequest` and `service_arrivals` receives/returns it; `OwnerEndpoint`/`ProducerEndpoint` own the public transfer. ABI version 3, request generation, `OwnerCore::pool_epoch`, immutable pool/slot trailer, and `WorkerFacts.epoch` guard identity. Startup allocates exactly two request and two orbit buffers at `max(644,64+8M)` and resize replaces all four; request/header/orbit/fact bytes are written before `post_message_with_transfer` or queue delivery, and credit rewrites the returned header. There is no GPU fence; detachment plus slot ownership and the bounded drain are the synchronization (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:864-875`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:121-178`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/reference.rs:413-530`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/wire.rs:6-40`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/wire.rs:87-176`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/wire.rs:387-430`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/wire.rs:433-600`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/endpoint.rs:158-188`).

**Proposed.** The cut is already real; its smallest engine port is an `OwnedEditBuffer` transport trait implemented by existing request slots, leaving ABI-3 orbit codecs as the Julibrot adapter. Before extraction, route same-thread ownership through `OwnerCore` so the two lowerings share the state machine rather than only an equivalence assertion.

### J-03 — owner/drain seam

**Verified.** App setters and reference arrival handling are the writers; `ViewerController::drain_hot`, `drain_main`, and direct app calls are readers. `ViewerOwner` owns staged/published `ViewerState`, navigation state, latest `u32` request generation, `u32` centre revision, and checked `u64` publication epoch; both drain names call the same `drain`, copy all HOT and MAIN fields, and increment the shared epoch. The owner uses no event queue and needs no fence because its `Cell` records are same-thread, but bignum navigation inside its non-copy navigation state can allocate (`crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:15-75`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:158-188`, `crates/labs/julibrot/worker/src/owner.rs:520-644`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/state.rs:1264-1357`).

**Proposed.** Make J-03 real by replacing `owner()`/`owner_mut()` exposure with controller methods over a narrow `ViewerDrainPort` whose results distinguish an unchanged HOT observation from a MAIN publication while preserving the current full-record bytes and epoch sequence. No graph types are required for that extraction.

### J-04 — graph-compilation seam

**Verified.** No scoped owner drain produces a node, dependency, dirty bit, or stable graph identity: the frame loop drains HOT, installs `PresentMain`, services worker arrivals, calls kernels, submits a scene, then acquires/submits a surface warp in one imperative refresh. The design reference itself marks stable resource/frame graphs as missing and round one names J-04 for a future compiler (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:1120-1365`; `docs/engine-design.md:257-269`, `docs/engine-design.md:296-320`).

**Proposed.** The smallest honest port is a pure `compile_viewer_drain(previous, next) -> ViewerWorkSet` adapter with stable Julibrot resource keys and ordered HOT, reference, kernel, scene, and presentation intents. It should initially be asserted against the existing trace and remain app-local; introducing a general DAG belongs after the miniature emits this record.

### J-05 — hot-publication seam

**Verified.** Every browser refresh can call `ViewerController::drain_hot`, construct `HotSlot::for_refresh(refresh_id, stride, epoch)`, and call `Presenter::write_hot` up to three times as newly accepted state changes the prepared extent or draft decision. Present owns the fixed three-slot allocation (`hot_ring_bytes`), the opaque index/dynamic offset, and the 288-byte `HotUniform`; versions are refresh id modulo three plus owner `u64` epoch. The only GPU write is `queue.write_buffer` to that slot. It has no separate fence; queue ordering makes it visible to the following scene or warp submission, whose own fence proves consumption (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:1148-1270`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/uniform.rs:7-18`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/uniform.rs:116-190`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/warp.rs:190-227`).

**Proposed.** Preserve the real cut and expose only `HotPublication { epoch, slot, bytes }` behind a fixed-identity writer; callers should not learn ring offsets. The immediate debt is to record the byte payload and write order in the oracle, not to change allocation or fencing.

### J-06 — scene-descriptor seam

**Verified.** `BrowserFrameLoop::install_main` is the concrete emitter: it selects main/backdrop whole grids and calls `Presenter::set_main(PresentMain)`. App owns selection policy; kernels owns each `EscapeGrid` span; present owns `PresentMain`, `SceneUniform`, `TileContentKey::VERSION=1`, `TileRenderKey::VERSION=1`, and `DescriptorCostLedger::ABI_VERSION=1`. The live arm clones span handles and writes one or two 160-byte scene uniforms at scene submission; the tile arm allocates and writes nothing and has no publication fence (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/submit.rs:25-105`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/contract.rs:27-73`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene/submit.rs:39-100`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene/submit.rs:133-148`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/tile.rs:64-87`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/tile.rs:141-218`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/tile.rs:333-366`).

**Proposed.** Make the partial seam real by defining one engine-neutral regional descriptor record in kernels, adding a whole-grid-to-one-region adapter, and making `set_main` consume a slice of those records. Retain `PresentMain` as the old arm until descriptor bytes, scene identity, and pixels agree.

### J-07 — kernel-job seam

**Verified.** `BrowserFrameLoop::submit_due_scene` directly chooses shallow versus perturbation and calls `JulibrotKernels::encode_shallow` or `encode_perturbation`; kernels owns plans, span/header allocations, cached dispatch templates, accepted reference identity, and the mutable `EscapeGrid`. Versions are owner epoch, refinement rung, reference generation, requested/delivered caps, and `DataSpan` generation. Allocation reserves one or an atomic pair of Final-capacity spans and three header sets; dispatch writes a kernel uniform, encodes scratch work and exact DATA copies, then app submits that encoder before present submits its scene. There is no kernel-specific fence; same-queue order places the later scene fence after the kernel copy (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop/browser/submit.rs:300-414`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:20-70`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:135-269`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:271-447`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:681-702`).

**Proposed.** Make J-07 real by adding a `KernelJob` enum whose whole-grid variant carries exactly today's arguments and whose tile variant wraps the existing `TileJob`; return an encoded publication receipt rather than mutating app-selected grid state. The first adapter must call the same methods so queue submissions and scene-fence order remain byte-for-byte observable.

### J-08 — value/reconstruction seam

**Verified.** Stage 0 names `PairedOutputSpanPlan`, `PairedOutputCompletion`, `PublishedTileOutputs`, and `DescriptorSamplePair`; it checks equal sample lengths, non-aliasing spans, and two-sided completion, with `S0` retaining the current value lanes and `S1` carrying lifted reconstruction. No scoped call outside `tile_job.rs` and `tile.rs` tests constructs these records, and `JulibrotKernels` has only one live output span per grid. Consequently there are no live allocations, GPU writes, completion fences, or published generations for reconstruction (`crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/tile_job.rs:378-470`, `crates/labs/julibrot/kernels/src/gpu.rs:20-27`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/tile.rs:313-330`).

**Proposed.** Complete the port by teaching `JulibrotKernels` to allocate two generation-tagged spans, encode value and reconstruction writes in one command transaction, and expose `PublishedTileOutputs` only behind the scene fence that follows both; first do this for one whole-grid descriptor view so no tiling/index policy enters stage 1.

### J-09 — geometry/projection seam

**Verified.** Scene submission is the caller: `SceneUniform::new` packages extent, refinement, cap, span directory index/logical length, plane, and source map; device scene code builds an index buffer per main/backdrop extent, binds the immutable heap group and the scene/HOT group, and draws the mesh. `Pose` supplies owner epoch and orbit generation; `DataSpan` supplies heap generation; indices are reallocated on extent changes, scene uniforms are rewritten regionally, and the enclosing scene command/fence covers the draw. CPU `project_scene_vertex_exact` is the present-owned full-chain oracle, but live geometry has no tile/header identity (`crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/uniform.rs:196-258`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene.rs:156-233`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene.rs:235-376`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/planner.rs:834-996`).

**Proposed.** Make the port complete by changing the draw input from an `EscapeGrid` plus implicit pose to `{ mesh_handle, value_span, reconstruction_span, pose_header, active_index }`, with a one-record adapter for the retained whole grid. Keep mesh construction and full-chain projection present-owned.

### J-10 — scene-publication seam

**Verified.** `Presenter::submit_scene` owns validation, chooses a free ledger texture, advances `u64 next_scene_id`, captures pose/centre revision/plane origin/precision and a clone of the grid in `PendingScene`, rewrites scene uniforms, renders, copies the census, clears and submits the four-byte scene fence, and arms its `FenceLedger`. `poll_scene` performs one cooperative poll, maps completion/refusal, promotes only a compatible pending scene, and publishes `SceneFrame` plus `PresentFacts`; scene texture, depth, census, and index allocations are extent-keyed and `texture_reallocations` records changed scene targets (`crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene/submit.rs:13-200`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/poll.rs:20-28`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/poll.rs:41-117`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/poll.rs:172-205`).

**Proposed.** The real seam needs only an engine-neutral `CompletedScene` view over the existing immutable identity and source pose, plus the fixed two-event poll return. Do not move ledger compatibility or fences into app.

### J-11 — reprojection/composition seam

**Verified.** `Presenter::write_hot` calls `Warp::reproject` against the retained `SceneFrame`, applies coverage/hold/lattice policy, and writes the plan into HOT; `Presenter::frame` chooses the retained source, allocates/resizes the transient value target as needed, uploads palette, encodes relief redraw or image warp, shades once into the app surface, optionally encodes readback, clears/submits a four-byte warp fence, and returns `FrameReceipt` under a monotonic `u64 warp_id`. `poll_warp` publishes completion/refusal; app presents only afterward. The current scene compositor draws backdrop and main with separate viewport depth ranges/stencil, so it is not the study's physical target-depth competition among independently reprojected views (`crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/warp.rs:44-227`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/warp.rs:268-476`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/poll.rs:119-170`, `crates/labs/julibrot/present/src/gpu/device/scene.rs:235-303`).

**Proposed.** Stage 1 makes this a real tile-capable port with one source: reconstruct every retained descriptor vertex, execute the complete requested target chain, write target depth and semantic value in one pass, and shade the winning value once. Preserve the whole-frame `WarpPlan` arm behind the same `FrameReceipt` and warp-fence interface.

### J-12 — surface seam

**Verified.** `BrowserFrameLoop::refresh` is the caller; `BrowserRuntime` owns the `wgpu::Surface`, configuration, device/queue, and `SurfaceState`. `SurfaceState` owns a single token keyed by `u32` app generation and at most one `PendingSurface` keyed by `u64` present warp id. Acquisition creates a `SurfaceTexture`; retention itself allocates nothing; matching completion optionally offers the texture to readback, calls `present`, and releases the token, while refusal/cancellation drops it. The warp fence is present-owned, `PresentEvent::WarpCompleted` is the handoff, and presentation lies after the measurement (`crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/runtime.rs:41-49`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/runtime.rs:248-329`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/surface.rs:9-137`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:1307-1357`, `crates/labs/julibrot/app/src/frame/loop.rs:1465-1508`).

**Proposed.** Keep the real ownership state intact and expose it behind `SurfacePort::acquire`, `retain`, and `resolve(PresentEvent)`; the port should return the existing present/drop/ignore action so round two can extract it without changing fence or presentation order.
