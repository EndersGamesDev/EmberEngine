# End Game castle

V9 continues the same run after the prison escape. The greatsword, dead warden and broken exit chain allow the far portcullis to lift. The player can then explore seven connected places and return through them: lower passage, great hall, grand stair, backyard garden, castle walls, tower and summit. Reaching the summit does not pause or end the game.

`end-game-core/src/layout.rs` owns the castle's measured solids and prop placements in metres. The renderer consumes those same floors, steps, landings and wall bounds. The route begins at the old basement exit around `(0, 0, -7)`, passes a 28 × 32 m hall, rises 6 m to the garden, reaches the wall walk at 14 m and ends at the 22 m tower lookout. The tower uses alternating flights and open landings rather than slabs across the active stairwell.

The controller has a 0.22 m body radius and 0.28 m step allowance. Stair risers are 0.25 m. Horizontal movement takes bounded substeps; support is selected below the reachable feet height, and vertical motion checks slab tops and undersides. Grounding drives jumping, guard availability and footstep sounds at every level. A small camera offset eases stair motion while collision and third-person feet retain their physical support height.

Generated Gothic supports, a dry courtyard fountain and a carved tower portal provide close architectural detail. `assets/end-game/v9/` records the SDXL/TRELLIS jobs, transformed bounds, RGB8 textures and exported hashes. The portal has separate jamb and lintel collision around its measured opening. The smaller openings in the decorative support cluster are not passages. Reproduction tools are under `tools/end-game/v9/`; large generation sources remain outside the checkout.

The engine registers each generated model once and reuses its mesh IDs. Resident texture storage and submitted triangle counts are separate budgets: visibility selection reduces submitted geometry but cannot release uploaded textures. V9 checks allow up to 136 MiB of resident texture storage including mipmaps and bound representative views to 260,000 submitted triangles. V10 permits 156 MiB and 300,000 submitted triangles to accommodate its generated enemies; repeated enemies share registered mesh IDs. Browser adaptive resolution remains available up to the existing 5120-pixel width cap; that cap is not a measured frame-rate claim.

For passive native inspection, set `EMBER_CAPTURE_PATH`, `END_GAME_SCENE=castle` and optional finite `END_GAME_X`, `END_GAME_Y`, `END_GAME_Z`, `END_GAME_YAW`, `END_GAME_PITCH`. Defaults place the player at `(0, 0, -24)` facing the great hall. `END_GAME_EXIT_OPEN` stages the portcullis from 0 to 1, and `END_GAME_THIRD_PERSON=1` shows the hero. `END_GAME_TIME` freezes environmental motion. These fixtures stage an escaped, equipped player and capture the engine scene without activating the window or driving workstation input.

V9 is retained as the unpopulated exploration release. V10 adds the encounters and escape route described below. Its masonry and props use measured collision, baked color textures and scalar material response; it does not simulate structural destruction or material microstructure. Automated traversal and passive scene inspection are separate from browser gameplay, physical controller/touch verification, audible playback and measured 5K frame pacing.

## The Last Seal — V10

The castle has eight regular enemies and the One-Eyed Castellan: sword and spear soldiers in the lower passage and great hall, hollow axe knights in the garden and on the walls, and the cyclops boss in the great hall. Each weapon has its own reach, windup, contact time, recovery and stamina cost. Attacks lock their facing before contact, check solid geometry and height, and leave recovery openings. The encounter coordinator permits one committed attacker at a time; other foes approach and hold distance. The Castellan alternates a guardable sweep with a clearly warned crushing slam that requires a dodge, and pursues faster after losing half his health.

Read the inscription at the great hall entrance using E, Square or Use. The journal in Menu keeps discovered clues. Defeat the Castellan and collect his dropped crown, awaken the Sun at the dry garden fountain, the Wolf on the western wall and the Bell at the tower summit, then present the crown at the west garden gate. The vows must be awakened in order; a premature later vow resets them with a hint. Already-awakened vows can be reread. The gate physically rises before the player can walk along its exterior path to the ending. Opening it alone does not complete the run.

The castle entry checkpoint restores health and stamina after death. Victories, clues, seal progress, collected crown, used shrines and gate state persist; living enemies return to their authored spawns. Three healing shrines each provide one full heal only while injured. There is no persistent browser save: reloading or choosing Restart begins a fresh prison run.

Generated character GLBs and audio provenance live in `assets/end-game/v10/`. Named rigid parts animate from core state, with separate grip-attached sword, spear, axe and maul models. Audio events use monotonic IDs and retained history so skipped render frames do not swallow story lines. Prerecorded speech has timed captions even when audio is unavailable or muted; menu pause preserves clip offsets. The ending voice can finish while the completed simulation is paused.

V10 carries the idle greatsword low on the right so the enemy and clue remain visible. The draw transition uses a lifted, swing-then-twist return with actual blade triangles checked against the two plinth stones. Guard and contact arcs retain their combat poses. The slam warning outlines the same forward sector used by damage, and a visible enemy's active windup takes priority in the HUD over a nearer waiting foe. Guard readiness uses that attack's stamina cost, including 32 for an axe chop and 38 for a Castellan sweep; an unblockable slam explicitly calls for a dodge.

Native `END_GAME_SCENE=enemy` selects a frozen generated enemy pose. `END_GAME_ENEMY_ID=0..8` selects the authored roster; `END_GAME_ENEMY_PHASE=idle|walk|attack|stagger|dead`, `END_GAME_ACTION_TIME`, `END_GAME_WALK_PHASE`, `END_GAME_WALK_BLEND` and `END_GAME_PHASE_TWO=1` set the state. The boss accepts `END_GAME_ENEMY_ATTACK=sweep|slam`. `END_GAME_ENEMY_DISTANCE` sets camera distance and `END_GAME_ENEMY_VIEW=side|back` changes the inspection angle. Castle fixtures also accept `END_GAME_SALLY_OPEN=0..1`, `END_GAME_SEALS=0..3`, `END_GAME_CROWN=1` and `END_GAME_ENEMIES=none`. These are passive inspection fixtures, not gameplay shortcuts in the browser.
